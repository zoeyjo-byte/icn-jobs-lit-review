# AI Deskilling as Structural Problem (2026)

## Key Findings
- AI creates capacity-hostile environments through:
  - Affordance shrinkage in digital environments
  - Embodiment deficit in social interactions
  - Volitional capacity erosion through automation

## Methodology
Philosophical analysis combining:
- Developmental perfectionism framework
- Skill philosophy (agential control + habituation)
- Social epistemology

## Implications
- Socio-technical systems must prioritize capacity-conducive design
- Critique of individual responsibility narratives
