## AI Deskilling as a Structural Problem (Ferdman, 2026)

### Key Findings
- AI systems create [[capacity-hostile-environments]] through:
  - Narrow affordance fields in digital interfaces
  - Disembodied interaction patterns
  - Automation of routine cognitive tasks

- Capacity cultivation requires:
  - Embodied, intersubjective habituation
  - Agential control over skill development
  - Developmental perfectionism framework

### Methodology
Philosophical analysis combining:
- Social epistemology
- Skill philosophy (agential control, habituation)
- Neo-Aristotelian flourishing theory
