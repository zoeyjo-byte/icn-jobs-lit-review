# AMA 2026 State of Marketing Careers Report

Published by the American Marketing Association, this report examines how marketing jobs, skills, and teams are shifting in the age of AI agents. The report identifies critical trends including the "seismic shifts" from content generation to agentic operations, the "rise of the AI-powered generalist," and the challenges facing entry-level marketers. While detailed content was not available in the source file, the table of contents reveals significant parallels to the market research industry's transformation, particularly regarding the [[value-chain-shift]] and [[entry-level-squeeze]]. The report's "AI Disruption Map" and "Skills Disruption Map" provide frameworks relevant to understanding AI's impact on professional roles.

## Key Structural Analysis
The report documents a fundamental repositioning of value creation through four interconnected shifts:
- **From Content Generation to Agentic Operations**: AI handles routine content production while humans focus on orchestrating AI agents and interpreting outputs
- **Executors Become Orchestrators**: Marketers evolve from task performers to AI workflow designers
- **Rise of the AI-Powered Generalist**: Hybrid roles demanding both domain expertise and AI fluency
- **Leaner, Meaner Pods**: Cross-functional teams of 3-5 specialists replacing traditional departmental structures

## Critical Skill Transitions
Identifies four future-proof marketing skills with direct market research parallels:
- **Experimental Mindset**: Rapid hypothesis testing through AI-aided iteration
- **Customer Focus**: Deep empathy for human needs beyond AI-generated personas
- **Strategic Processing**: Synthesizing AI outputs into actionable business insights
- **Intelligence, Artificially**: Human judgment in AI-augmented decision environments
