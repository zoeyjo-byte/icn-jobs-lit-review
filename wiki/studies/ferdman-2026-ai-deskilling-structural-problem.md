# [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]

## Publication Details
**Journal**: AI & Society (2026) 41:3001-3013  
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-025-02686-z  
**Author**: Avigail Ferdman (Technion - Israel Institute of Technology)  
**Published**: November 5, 2025  

## Abstract
Many artificial intelligence tools replace or stand to replace human activity through automated decision-making, recommender systems, and content generation. As AI increasingly replaces valuable human activity, it risks deskilling humans of their core capacities. This paper argues for applying a structural perspective to this phenomenon, introducing 'capacity-hostile environments' to identify where AI mediation impedes human capacity cultivation. The analysis moves beyond individual responsibility, demonstrating how AI creates systemic conditions that inhibit capacity development by undermining the habituation process. Drawing on skill philosophy and social epistemology, the paper argues capacity cultivation requires acquiring agential control through gradual habituation, which depends on learning from others and shared understanding of skill value. AI mediation risks undermining conditions for capacity habituation, leading to capacity impoverishment. The paper calls for evaluating AI applications based on their conduciveness to capacity cultivation and emphasizes society's obligation to foster capacity-conducive environments.

## Core Framework

### Developmental Perfectionism
A neo-Aristotelian approach where human flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life. Humans exercise capacities in activities yielding intrinsically valuable outcomes like knowledge, beauty, morality, and friendship. The competent exercise of human capacities manifests achievement of these intrinsically valuable goods, making the capacities themselves intrinsically valuable.

### Four Core Human Capacity Categories
1. **Epistemic capacities**: Theoretical rationality (thinking, considering reasons, forming beliefs) and practical rationality (phronesis/wisdom): doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason.
2. **Social capacities**: Moral capacity and capacities for developing meaningful relationships of friendship and love.
3. **Creative capacities**: Making unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas, transforming conceptual spaces without relying on luck or mechanical procedures.
4. **Volitional capacity**: Meta-capacity to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow to achieve intrinsically valuable goals.

### Capacity-Skill Framework
Two key properties distinguish capacities as skills:

1. **Agential control**: Ability to adjust action execution as performance unfolds. Skills require control over initiating tasks, whereas habits are triggered by environmental cues.
2. **Habituation**: Gradual skill development through practice, inculcation via attunement to individual cases, requiring encountering and overcoming disappointment and failure.

Habituation has two critical components:
- **Intersubjectivity**: Skill development requires mentors who instill both mechanical aspects and the value of the skill, creating shared valuing.
- **Embodiment**: Successful habituation requires embodied 'participatory sense-making,' crucial for recognizing others as moral agents and forming relationships.

## Structural Analysis of Deskilling

### Capacity-Hostile vs. Conducive Environments
- **Capacity-hostile environments**: Restrict, limit, or create narrow fields of affordances for capacity development. Afford shallow skilling while restricting fuller development.
- **Capacity-conducive environments**: Encourage capacity development through rich opportunities for embodied interaction and shared valuing.

### Three Evaluation Axes for AI Systems
1. **Field of affordances**: Action possibilities provided by the environment
2. **Embodied engagement**: Level of physical presence required
3. **Intersubjectivity**: Opportunities for shared valuing through social interaction

### AI Automation Risks
- Automating routine tasks deprives users of practice opportunities needed to strengthen 'cognitive musculature'
- Offloading human activity reduces opportunities to exercise volitional capacity
- Disembodied interactions make it harder to form meaningful relationships
- Narrow affordance fields discourage users from finding compensatory pathways

## Artificial Personal Assistants Case Study

### Life-Planning Analysis
APAs are increasingly used for life-planning activities (organizing life, finding purpose, determining values). The paper distinguishes:
- **Plan-forming**: Requires exercising agential control over settling (deciding between options) and coordination functions of planning
- **Plan-following**: Responding to environmental triggers (APA outputs) without initiating planning

### Capacity-Hostile Mechanisms
1. **Reduced agential control**: Users respond to APA recommendations rather than initiating planning
2. **Diminished habituation**: Offloading planning reduces opportunities to develop practical wisdom through real-world experience
3. **Undermined intersubjectivity**: APA mediation decreases embodied, intersubjective interaction with others
4. **Responsibility displacement**: Users shift blame to APAs when plans fail, losing self-criticism opportunities

## Market Research Implications

### Deskilling Risks
- AI tools automating routine research tasks may deprive researchers of opportunities to develop analytical reasoning through habituation
- Fully automated survey analysis systems may create capacity-hostile environments by removing opportunities for agential control
- Remote research platforms eliminating embodied interaction with participants undermine social capacity development

### Structural Solutions
- Design research workflows maintaining capacity-conducive environments
- Preserve opportunities for researchers to exercise agential control over research planning
- Create mentorship structures supporting intersubjective habituation of research capacities
- Balance AI automation with embodied, collaborative research practices
