# Ferdman (2026): AI Deskilling is a Structural Problem

## Study Overview

Avigail Ferdman's 2026 paper in *AI & Society* presents a structural analysis of how AI systems can undermine human capacities through systemic environmental conditions rather than individual failings. Published November 5, 2025, this research introduces the critical distinction between **capacity-hostile environments** and **capacity-conducive environments**, arguing that AI's influence creates systemic conditions that inhibit the development and exercise of core human capacities.

## Key Arguments

Ferdman challenges the individual responsibility framework that dominates discussions of AI-induced deskilling, demonstrating how AI mediation creates environments that actively undermine the **habituation process** required for capacity development. The paper identifies four essential components of capacity cultivation:

1. **Agential control**: The ability to adjust execution of actions as performance unfolds
2. **Habituation**: Gradual skill development through practice and attunement to individual cases
3. **Intersubjectivity**: Learning from others through shared understanding of skill value
4. **Embodiment**: Physical presence and sensory experience in skill development

The study argues that AI systems like [[artificial-personal-assistants]] risk creating **capacity-hostile environments** by:
- Automating routine tasks that provide essential practice for capacity development
- Reducing opportunities for embodied interaction critical for social/moral capacities
- Undermining intersubjective learning through disembodied mediation
- Discouraging the exercise of **agential control** through habit-forming triggers

## Methodology

Ferdman employs philosophical analysis grounded in **developmental perfectionism** (Kauppinen 2025), examining how AI systems affect the cultivation of four core human capacities:
- Epistemic capacities (theoretical and practical rationality)
- Social capacities (moral reasoning and relationship formation)
- Creative capacities (novel idea combination)
- Volitional capacity (willpower to pursue intrinsically valuable goals)

The paper analyzes AI's impact through case studies of **artificial personal assistants** (APAs), showing how these systems can narrow the field of affordances for capacity development.

## Implications for Market Research

This research has profound implications for the insights industry:
- AI tools that automate data interpretation may undermine researchers' **agential control** over analytical reasoning
- Remote research methodologies that reduce embodied interaction threaten **embodiment-in-skilling** for social capacities
- Over-reliance on AI for insight generation risks creating **capacity-hostile environments** where junior researchers miss critical habituation opportunities
- The industry must design **capacity-conducive environments** that preserve opportunities for intersubjective learning through mentorship
