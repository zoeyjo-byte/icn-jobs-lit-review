# Creative Capacity

The **creative capacity** is the human ability to make unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas, transform conceptual spaces, and respond innovatively to novel situations. It is not merely a product of luck, accidents, or mechanical procedures, but a cultivated skill that requires deliberate practice and agential control.

## Role in Human Flourishing
Creativity is essential for adapting to unexpected events, revising plans, and generating new meanings in both personal and social contexts. In **[[life-planning]]**, for example, executing a plan requires the creative capacity to reconsider and respond to surprises, which are often sources of value and growth.

## AI and Creative Deskilling
When AI tools automate content generation, problem-solving, or decision-making, they risk replacing the effortful process of creative synthesis with algorithmic outputs. This can lead to a shallow exercise of creativity, where users become accustomed to consuming rather than generating novel combinations. Over time, this may diminish the ability to transform conceptual spaces independently, contributing to **[[capacity-hostile-environments]]** that discourage innovative thinking and adaptive problem-solving.
