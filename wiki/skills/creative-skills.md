## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Creative skills involve generating novel ideas, making unfamiliar combinations of familiar concepts, and transforming conceptual spaces. These skills enable innovation and adaptation in response to novel situations.

Ferdman's analysis adds:
- Creative capacities are essential when executing plans requires adaptation to unexpected events
- Creativity involves transforming conceptual spaces without relying merely on luck or mechanical procedures
- The creative process requires habituation through practice, encountering and overcoming disappointment
- AI tools that provide ready-made solutions reduce opportunities to develop creative problem-solving

Threats from AI in market research:
- AI-generated insights may reduce researchers' opportunities to develop creative interpretation skills
- Automated analysis tools may truncate the creative habituation process
- Over-reliance on AI for idea generation limits exposure to unexpected findings that spark creativity
- Research workflows that prioritize efficiency over exploration diminish creative capacity development

Preservation strategies:
- Design research workflows that include open-ended exploration phases
- Create opportunities for researchers to encounter and respond to unexpected findings
- Balance AI-assisted analysis with human-led creative interpretation
- Structure team brainstorming sessions that encourage unfamiliar combinations of ideas
- Provide researchers with time for unstructured creative thinking

Market research applications:
- Innovation labs within research firms can preserve creative capacity development
- Client presentations should include space for creative interpretation beyond AI-generated insights
- Junior researchers need exposure to the full research process to develop creative capacities
- Research firms should maintain spaces for unstructured creative thinking separate from AI tools
