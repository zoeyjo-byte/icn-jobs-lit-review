# Creative Skills

Human capacities related to generating novel ideas, making unfamiliar combinations of familiar concepts, and transforming conceptual spaces. These skills enable innovation and adaptation through non-algorithmic thinking that AI cannot replicate, forming the foundation for breakthrough research design and insight generation.

## Core Components
- **Conceptual Blending**: Synthesizing disparate ideas into new frameworks
- **Analogical Reasoning**: Drawing insights from unexpected domains
- **Ambiguity Navigation**: Working productively with incomplete information
- **Playful Experimentation**: Testing unconventional approaches without predefined outcomes

## AI Context
While AI accelerates idea generation, human creativity determines:
- Which combinations have meaningful research implications
- When to abandon unproductive conceptual paths
- How to frame questions that yield valuable insights
