# Epistemic Capacities

## Expanded Definition
Includes theoretical rationality (critical thinking, belief formation) and practical rationality (phronesis/wisdom) requiring:
- Habituation through embodied practice
- Intersubjective mentorship
- Environmental affordances for growth

## Deskilling Risks
- AI automation reducing cognitive musculature development (Rinta-Kahila et al. 2023)
- Healthcare AI diminishing diagnostic reasoning (NHS England 2023)
