# Social Skills

Human capacities related to forming meaningful relationships, understanding others' perspectives, and participating in communal life. These skills are essential for ethical research design, participant engagement, and translating insights into human-centered business actions, with embodiment requirements that AI cannot replicate.

## Foundational Elements
- **Relational Attunement**: Sensing unspoken participant cues during interactions
- **Moral Discernment**: Navigating ethical dilemmas in research design
- **Contextual Empathy**: Understanding perspectives within cultural frameworks
- **Shared Meaning Creation**: Co-constructing insights with stakeholders

## AI Vulnerabilities
Social skills degrade in capacity-hostile environments when:
- AI handles initial participant screening
- Automated tools replace human moderation
- Quantitative metrics override qualitative understanding
