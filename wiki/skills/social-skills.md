# Social Skills

First Observed: 2026-07-25

Social skills represent the human capacities related to forming meaningful relationships, understanding others' perspectives, and participating in communal life. According to Ferdman's analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], these include moral capacity as well as social and caring capacities for developing meaningful relationships of friendship and love.

## In Market Research Context

In the market research industry, social skills are critical for:

- **Client relationship management**: Building trust with clients through understanding their needs and constraints
- **Stakeholder communication**: Translating complex research findings into actionable insights for diverse audiences
- **Team collaboration**: Working effectively with cross-functional teams to design and execute research projects
- **Ethical research practices**: Maintaining integrity in data collection and interpretation
- **Cultural sensitivity**: Understanding diverse perspectives when conducting global research

## Threats from AI

AI systems can create [[capacity-hostile-environments]] for social skills when they:

- Replace human-to-human interaction with AI-mediated communication
- Reduce opportunities for embodied interaction necessary for developing moral agency
- Create disembodied environments that make it harder to form meaningful professional relationships
- Encourage researchers to become plan-followers rather than plan-formers in client engagements
- Undermine the intersubjective dimension of skill development by removing human mentors

## Maintaining Social Skills

To preserve social skills in an AI-augmented research environment:

- Prioritize face-to-face interactions for critical client discussions and complex problem-solving
- Create structured opportunities for embodied, intersubjective learning through mentorship programs
- Design AI tools that augment rather than replace human connection in research processes
- Maintain spaces for shared valuing of social capacities through team reflection and discussion
- Ensure researchers have opportunities to develop [[agential-control]] in client relationships

See [[embodiment-in-skilling]], [[intersubjectivity-in-skilling]], and [[capacity-conducive-environments]] for related concepts.
