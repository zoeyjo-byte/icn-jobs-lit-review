## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Social skills encompass the human capacities related to forming meaningful relationships, understanding others' perspectives, and participating in communal life. These skills include moral capacity and relational capacity for developing friendships and love.

Ferdman's analysis adds:
- Social capacities require embodied interaction for full development
- The return of the gaze in embodied interaction enables spontaneous recognition of others as moral agents
- Disembodied interaction requires more mental effort to attribute moral agency to others
- Social capacities develop through intersubjective habituation with mentors and exemplars

Threats from AI in market research:
- Remote research methods reduce opportunities for embodied client interaction
- AI tools that replace human-to-human communication diminish social capacity development
- Digital collaboration platforms create disembodied environments that make relationship formation harder
- Information bubbles created by algorithms undermine moral reasoning necessary for ethical research

Preservation strategies:
- Balance remote research with in-person client interactions
- Design research workflows that preserve opportunities for embodied interaction
- Create mentorship programs that facilitate intersubjective learning of social capacities
- Structure team collaboration to include face-to-face elements
- Train researchers in ethical reasoning through case studies and discussion

Market research applications:
- Client workshops should include opportunities for embodied interaction
- Research training should emphasize the social dimensions of research work
- Firms should evaluate whether their AI tools support or undermine researchers' social capacities
- Ethical review processes should preserve opportunities for moral reasoning development
