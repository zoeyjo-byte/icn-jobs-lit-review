# Epistemic Skills

Human capacities related to knowledge acquisition, critical thinking, reasoning, and understanding, including theoretical rationality and practical rationality (phronesis). These skills enable researchers to evaluate AI outputs, identify biases, and apply contextual judgment—forming the foundation for trustworthy insights in AI-augmented workflows.

## Core Dimensions
- **Critical Evaluation**: Assessing AI outputs for validity and relevance
- **Contextual Integration**: Situating findings within broader business landscapes
- **Epistemic Humility**: Recognizing knowledge limitations in complex systems
- **Strategic Sensemaking**: Transforming data into actionable understanding

## AI Enhancement Points
Epistemic skills determine when to:
- Override AI recommendations based on domain expertise
- Identify edge cases where AI reasoning fails
- Translate technical outputs into strategic implications
