## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Volitional skills refer to the human capacities related to exerting effort, overcoming difficulty, persevering, and growing to achieve intrinsically valuable goals. These skills are treated as meta-capacities necessary for developing all other capacities.

Ferdman's analysis adds:
- Volitional capacity is required for developing and exercising all other human capacities
- It enables agents to initiate plan-forming rather than defaulting to plan-following
- Without volitional capacity, agents become dependent on external triggers rather than initiating activities
- Developing volitional capacity requires overcoming challenges and persisting through difficulty

Threats from AI in market research:
- AI tools that automate routine tasks reduce opportunities to exercise volitional capacity
- Over-reliance on AI for decision-making undermines the ability to initiate research activities
- Convenience of AI tools may erode perseverance in facing research challenges
- APA-like tools that constantly provide answers diminish opportunities to develop volitional capacity

Preservation strategies:
- Design research workflows that preserve challenging aspects requiring effort
- Create opportunities for researchers to initiate research questions rather than just execute plans
- Structure assignments to include appropriate levels of difficulty that require perseverance
- Balance AI automation with human-led activities that demand volitional effort
- Recognize and reward research achievements that required significant perseverance

Market research applications:
- Research firms should design career paths that gradually increase challenges requiring volitional capacity
- Junior researchers need assignments that require effortful problem-solving
- AI implementation should avoid eliminating the need for effortful research activities
- Performance evaluations should consider development of volitional capacity alongside technical skills
