# [[volitional-skills]]

## 2026-08-26 Update

Human capacities related to exerting effort, overcoming difficulty, persevering, and growing to achieve intrinsically valuable goals. Expanded with detailed analysis from Ferdman's structural perspective on capacity development, including meta-capacity role, AI threats in research workflows, and market-specific preservation strategies as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

### Characteristics
- Involves exerting effort and overcoming difficulty in research tasks
- Requires perseverance through analytical challenges
- Enables growth toward intrinsically valuable research goals
- Functions as a meta-capacity necessary for developing other research skills
- Develops through encountering and overcoming disappointment and failure

### Meta-Capacity Role
Volitional skills are foundational to all other research capacities because:
- Developing any research skill requires effort and determination
- Overcoming analytical challenges is essential for skill growth
- Perseverance enables progression through research expertise stages
- Growth toward intrinsically valuable research goals requires sustained willpower
- Without volitional skills, other research capacities cannot be fully developed

### AI Threats in Research Workflows
Ferdman identifies specific AI-related threats:
- **Automated task completion**: Tools that replace human effort in routine research activities
- **Instant insight generation**: Systems that provide ready-made interpretations without requiring perseverance
- **Template-based workflows**: Structures that reduce need for overcoming research challenges
- **Predictive analytics**: Platforms that minimize unexpected events requiring volitional response

### Market Research Examples
- AI-powered survey analysis tools that complete data interpretation without requiring researchers to struggle with complex patterns
- Automated reporting systems that generate insights without requiring perseverance through analytical challenges
- Standardized research templates that reduce need for overcoming methodological difficulties
- Predictive analytics platforms that minimize unexpected findings requiring volitional adaptation

### Preservation Strategies
- Design AI tools that preserve opportunities for effortful engagement
- Create workflows requiring researchers to overcome challenges before accessing AI assistance
- Implement training programs emphasizing perseverance through research difficulties
- Develop organizational practices that value effortful skill development
- Balance efficiency gains with opportunities to exercise volitional skills

### Connection to Capacity Development
Volitional skills develop through:
- Encountering and overcoming disappointment and failure in research
- Gradual habituation involving inculcation through continued attunement
- Opportunities to exercise agential control over initiating research activities
- Embodied, intersubjective learning environments
- Shared valuing of perseverance through research mentorship

Ferdman's framework emphasizes that preserving volitional skills in market research requires structural solutions maintaining opportunities for researchers to engage in effortful work. Organizations must balance AI efficiency with the need for spaces where researchers can develop perseverance and overcome challenges. The value of market research insights depends not just on speed of delivery but on the volitional skill exercise that enables researchers to push through difficult analytical problems. Capacity-conducive research environments intentionally preserve opportunities for effortful engagement, recognizing that without volitional skills, researchers cannot fully develop the other capacities essential for high-quality research. This meta-capacity is particularly vulnerable to AI-induced deskilling because AI tools often specifically target the automation of effortful research tasks.
