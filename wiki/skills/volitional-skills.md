# Volitional Skills

First Observed: 2026-07-25

Volitional skills represent the human capacity to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow in order to achieve intrinsically valuable goals. As Ferdman explains in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this functions as a meta-capacity necessary for developing all other capacities, since developing and exercising any human capacity is difficult and requires willpower.

## In Market Research Context

In the market research industry, volitional skills are critical for:

- **Project management**: Maintaining focus and momentum through complex, multi-stage research projects
- **Quality assurance**: Persisting through detailed validation processes to ensure research integrity
- **Deadline management**: Exerting effort to meet time-sensitive business needs
- **Overcoming research obstacles**: Navigating unexpected challenges in data collection or analysis
- **Professional development**: Committing to ongoing learning and skill enhancement

## Threats from AI

AI systems can create [[capacity-hostile-environments]] for volitional skills when they:

- Automate challenging aspects of research that previously required perseverance and effort
- Reduce opportunities to develop willpower through overcoming research difficulties
- Create environments where researchers become plan-followers rather than plan-formers
- Undermine the development of practical wisdom by removing opportunities to exercise judgment in unexpected scenarios
- Replace the gradual habituation process with instant AI-generated solutions

## Maintaining Volitional Skills

To preserve volitional skills in an AI-augmented research environment:

- Design research processes that maintain appropriate challenges requiring human effort and perseverance
- Create capacity-conducive environments that encourage researchers to exercise [[agential-control]] over project direction
- Ensure opportunities for researchers to encounter and overcome meaningful research obstacles
- Maintain spaces for developing practical wisdom through handling unexpected research scenarios
- Balance AI augmentation with opportunities for researchers to develop their [[volitional-capacity]]

See [[agential-control]], [[habituation-process]], and [[plan-forming-vs-plan-following]] for related concepts.
