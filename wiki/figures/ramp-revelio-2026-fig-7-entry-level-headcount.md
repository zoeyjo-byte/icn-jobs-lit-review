# ramp-revelio-2026-fig-7-entry-level-headcount

![Entry-Level Headcount](figures/ramp-revelio-ai-jobs-impact-2026/fig-7.jpg)

## Description
This event study figure shows the impact of AI adoption on entry-level headcount for high-intensity adopters compared to later adopters in the same intensity group.

## Key Data Points
- **Month of adoption (0)**: ~0.5% increase in entry-level headcount
- **3 months after adoption**: ~2.5% increase
- **6 months after adoption**: ~7.0% increase
- **12 months after adoption**: ~12.0% increase
- **18 months after adoption**: ~15.0% increase
- **24 months after adoption**: ~18.0% increase

## Main Findings
- High-intensity AI adopters increase entry-level hiring following adoption
- Entry-level headcount rises 12.0% by 12 months after adoption
- The growth pattern is similar to total headcount growth, suggesting entry-level positions are part of the broader expansion
- This counters the view that intensive AI adoption is already reducing junior employment

## Significance
This finding challenges the "entry-level squeeze" narrative by showing that firms making substantial AI investments are actually expanding their entry-level positions. It suggests AI may be creating new opportunities for early-career professionals rather than eliminating their traditional training ground.
