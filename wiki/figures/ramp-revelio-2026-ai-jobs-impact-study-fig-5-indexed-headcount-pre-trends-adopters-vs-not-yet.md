# Figure 5: Indexed Headcount and Pre-Trends: AI Adopters vs. Not-Yet Adopters

![Indexed Headcount and Pre-Trends](figures/ramp-revelio-2026-ai-jobs-impact-study/fig-5.jpg)

## Description
This event study chart compares indexed total headcount for AI adopters versus later adopters (not-yet adopters) in the same eventual intensity group. Unlike Figure 4, the pre-treatment paths track closely, with both series aligned at 100 in event month -1. This alignment supports the parallel-trends assumption needed for valid causal inference, demonstrating that comparing earlier and later adopters within intensity groups provides a more appropriate counterfactual than using never-adopters.

## Key Insights
- Pre-treatment headcount paths are nearly identical for adopters and not-yet adopters
- Divergence begins approximately 6 months after adoption
- Growth differential reaches 10% by 18 months post-adoption
- Validates the Callaway-Sant'Anna methodology for staggered adoption designs
