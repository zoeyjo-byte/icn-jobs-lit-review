# ramp-revelio-2026-fig-1-ai-adoption-benchmarks

![AI Adoption Benchmarks](ramp-revelio-ai-jobs-impact-2026/fig-1.jpg)

## Description
This figure benchmarks the AI adoption rate in the linked Ramp-Revelio analytical panel against public survey estimates. It shows the percentage of firms adopting AI across different data sources from July 2021 to January 2026.

## Key Elements
- **Ramp/Revelio sample**: Shows paid AI adoption in the linked analytical panel (peaks around 60-70%)
- **Atlanta Fed SBU estimate**: Executive survey estimate from the Atlanta Fed Survey of Business Uncertainty
- **U.S. Census Estimate**: Nationally representative estimate from the U.S. Census Bureau's Business Trends and Outlook Survey
- **Yotzov et al. (2026) estimate**: Executive survey estimate from Yotzov et al.'s multinational survey

## Main Findings
- Different data sources show dramatically different AI adoption rates
- Executive surveys (Atlanta Fed, Yotzov) report higher adoption (69-78%) than Census data (17-20%)
- The Ramp-Revelio paid-adoption series is closer to executive survey estimates than to Census benchmarks
- Adoption rates increased steadily from 2021 through 2026 across all measures

## Significance
This figure illustrates why "AI adoption" does not have a single empirical rate. Different data sources capture different margins of use:
- Executive surveys capture broad organizational use
- Ramp-Revelio captures adoption that leaves a payment trace
- Census surveys are broad and nationally representative but sensitive to question wording
