# Figure 6: Total Headcount Event Study

![Total Headcount Event Study](ramp-revelio-2026-ai-jobs-impact-study/fig-6.jpg)

## Description
This event study chart shows the employment impact of high-intensity AI adoption over time. High-intensity adopters (top PEPM tercile) experience gradually increasing headcount growth, with effects becoming statistically significant around 6 months post-adoption. By month 18, headcount is 32% higher (0.277 log points) compared to later adopters in the same intensity group, reaching 57% higher (0.452 log points) by month 24, though with wider confidence intervals in later periods.

## Key Insights
- High-intensity AI adoption drives 10.2% total headcount growth over 24 months
- Effects follow a learning curve, emerging gradually after adoption
- Significant growth begins at 6-12 months as firms establish best practices
- Low-intensity adopters show no statistically significant headcount changes
