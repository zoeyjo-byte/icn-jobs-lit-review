# Sunsama

**Sunsama** is a scheduling and productivity application that integrates tasks from multiple software sources, syncs with calendars, and intelligently reschedules tasks when overtime occurs. It is frequently cited as an example of an APA-like tool that prompts users to prioritize work, link tasks to long-term objectives, and journal daily accomplishments.

## APA-Like Features
Sunsama exemplifies how traditional productivity tools are evolving into AI-mediated assistants. By displaying tasks, prompting prioritization, and guiding users through daily planning, it mirrors the functions of **[[artificial-personal-assistants]]**. While designed to enhance organization, such tools risk transforming users into plan-followers rather than plan-formers, potentially undermining the exercise of **[[epistemic-capacities]]** and **[[volitional-capacity]]** involved in self-directed life-planning.
