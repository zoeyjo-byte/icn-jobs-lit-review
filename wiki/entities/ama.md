# AMA

American Marketing Association, the premier professional association for marketing practitioners and academics. Founded in 1937, AMA provides research, training, and community resources for over 30,000 members globally, with significant influence on marketing education standards and industry practices.

## Research Contributions
AMA publishes foundational industry reports including:
- *Journal of Marketing* (flagship academic publication)
- *Marketing News* (industry practice insights)
- State of Marketing Careers Report series

## Market Research Relevance
AMA's frameworks directly inform insights practices through:
- Shared methodologies for customer segmentation
- Ethical guidelines for participant engagement
- Cross-industry benchmarking standards
