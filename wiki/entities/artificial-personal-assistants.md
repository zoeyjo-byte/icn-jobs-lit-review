# [[artificial-personal-assistants]]

## Definition

AI systems based on Large Language Models designed to assist with personal organization, life planning, and decision-making, with critical implications for capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These tools can create [[capacity-hostile-environments]] when they encourage users to become plan-followers rather than plan-formers.

## Key Features

- **Adaptive personalization**: Tailor advice based on user's responses, habits, and preferences
- **Constant availability**: Function as an "always-on sounding board" with subject-matter expertise
- **Non-judgmental interaction**: Willing to listen without judgment (Ma et al. 2023)
- **Multi-functional integration**: Combine capabilities as social companion, moral advisor, life coach, and personal butler

## Usage Patterns

Research shows significant adoption for life-planning activities:

- Second and third highest uses of LLMs are for "organizing my life" and "finding purpose" (Zao-Sanders 2025)
- Users rely on APAs to determine values, overcome roadblocks, and seek advice on next steps
- Heavy users utilize LLMs for social validation, self-regulation, and interpersonal guidance (Kim et al. 2025a)

## Capacity Impact Analysis

### Negative Effects

1. **Undermines plan-forming**: Encourages habitual response to AI recommendations rather than initiating planning activities
2. **Reduces practical wisdom development**: Limits real-world experience necessary for phronesis
3. **Weakens volitional capacity**: Removes challenges that require perseverance and effort
4. **Diminishes intersubjective habituation**: Decreases opportunities for shared valuing of capacities through human relationships

### Example: Sunsama Scheduling App

"Each morning, Sunsama displays tasks synced from various software, alongside your calendar and unfinished tasks from yesterday. It prompts you to prioritize today's work and to link tasks to your objectives. Sunsama then schedules tasks logically into your next available time slot, intelligently rescheduling them when other tasks run into overtime. The app reminds you throughout the day when it's time for meetings or breaks."

This automation of routine planning tasks deprives users of opportunities to "practice their judgment and strengthen their cognitive musculature" (Lee et al. 2025).

## Design Considerations

To avoid creating capacity-hostile environments, APAs should:

- Preserve opportunities for user initiation of planning activities
- Encourage embodied interaction with other humans
- Gradually reduce assistance as user competence develops (like training wheels)
- Provide space for user responsibility when plans fail
