# [[artificial-personal-assistants]]

## Definition
AI systems based on Large Language Models designed to assist with personal organization, life planning, and decision-making, with critical implications for capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These tools can create capacity-hostile environments when they encourage users to become plan-followers rather than plan-formers.

## Features and Usage Patterns
- Adapt to individual user's needs, habits, and preferences
- Provide 24/7 availability as an 'always-on sounding board'
- Offer subject-matter expertise across multiple domains
- Used for 'organizing life' and 'finding purpose' (second and third highest LLM uses)
- Provide social validation, self-regulation, and interpersonal guidance
- Configured with preferred interaction tone (taskmaster to cheerleader)

## Capacity Impact
- Encourage plan-following rather than plan-forming
- Reduce opportunities for agential control over life planning
- Undermine development of practical wisdom through real-world experience
- Decrease embodied, intersubjective interactions necessary for capacity development
- Create narrow fields of affordances for life planning activities
- Diminish users' sense of personal competence through over-reliance

## Market Research Implications
- APAs used in research design may undermine researchers' capacity to form research plans
- Over-reliance on AI for insight generation risks deskilling epistemic capacities
- The convenience of APAs may limit researchers' opportunities to develop practical wisdom
- Research organizations should evaluate APA tools for their conduciveness to capacity cultivation
- APAs that replace researcher judgment create capacity-hostile research environments

## Mitigation Strategies
- Use APAs as supplements rather than replacements for human planning
- Design research workflows that preserve opportunities for plan-forming
- Create structured opportunities for embodied interaction among research teams
- Implement mentorship programs to preserve intersubjective learning
- Evaluate APA tools based on their support for agential control and habituation

## Examples
- **Sunsama**: Scheduling app that plans users' days, schedules tasks, and provides reminders
- **Research-focused APAs**: Tools that generate survey questions, analyze data, and create reports
- **Personal coaching APAs**: Systems providing guidance on career development and research goals
