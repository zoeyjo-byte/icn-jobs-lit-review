# Artificial Personal Assistants (APAs)

**Artificial Personal Assistants (APAs)** are AI-driven systems, typically powered by Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, designed to adapt to individual user needs, tailor advice, and provide constant availability. They function as sounding boards, life coaches, moral advisors, and personal butlers.

## Primary Use Cases
Recent reports indicate that life-planning, organizing daily activities, and finding purpose are among the highest uses of LLMs. Users rely on APAs to:
- Determine and define personal values
- Get past roadblocks and reframe problems
- Seek advice on self-development and next steps
- Provide social validation and interpersonal guidance

## Risks and Concerns
While APAs offer convenience and non-judgmental support, over-reliance poses significant risks to human capacity cultivation:
- **Deskilling**: Offloading planning and decision-making reduces opportunities to practice judgment and strengthen cognitive musculature.
- **Plan-Follower vs. Plan-Former**: APAs invite users to follow AI-generated plans rather than initiate settling and coordinating functions, diminishing **[[agential-control]]**.
- **Capacity-Hostile Design**: Constant availability and predictive prompts narrow the **[[affordance-shrinkage|field of affordances]]**, discouraging users from seeking alternative avenues for growth and embodied social interaction.
