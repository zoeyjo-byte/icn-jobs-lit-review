# [[artificial-personal-assistants]]

## Definition

AI systems based on **Large Language Models** designed to assist with personal organization, life planning, and decision-making, with critical implications for capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These tools can create **[[capacity-hostile-environments]]** when they encourage users to become **[[plan-following]]** rather than **[[plan-forming]]** agents.

## Key Features

- **Always-on availability**: "An always-on sounding board with whatever subject-matter expertise you need" (Samuel 2025)
- **Personalization**: Adapts to individual user's needs, habits, and preferences
- **Non-judgmental interaction**: Willing to listen without judgment
- **Multi-domain expertise**: Functions as "virtual experts in different areas" (Altman 2024)
- **Integration capability**: Can be combined with other AI tools for comprehensive assistance

## Examples

- **Scheduling assistants**: Like Sunsama, which "displays tasks synced from various software... prompts you to prioritize today's work... schedules tasks logically into your next available time slot"
- **Life-coaching tools**: Providing advice on values, purpose, and self-development
- **Decision-support systems**: Offering recommendations for daily choices and long-term planning
- **Social validation platforms**: Providing interpersonal guidance and relationship advice

## Capacity Impact Analysis

Ferdman's research reveals significant risks:

- **Undermines agential control**: By providing complete plans rather than scaffolding
- **Reduces self-competence**: "Overreliance on APAs could lead to a reduced sense of personal competence"
- **Encourages plan-following**: Users respond to AI outputs rather than initiating planning
- **Narrows affordance field**: Decreases opportunities for small-scale decision-making that builds capacity
- **Disrupts habituation**: Deprives users of routine opportunities to practice judgment

## Design Considerations for Capacity-Conducive APAs

- Preserve space for **[[agential-control]]** by offering options rather than directives
- Support **[[habituation-process]]** through graduated assistance rather than complete solutions
- Maintain opportunities for **[[intersubjectivity-in-skilling]]** with human mentors
- Encourage **[[embodiment-in-skilling]]** by requiring physical presence for critical decisions
- Include mechanisms for users to take increasing responsibility over time
