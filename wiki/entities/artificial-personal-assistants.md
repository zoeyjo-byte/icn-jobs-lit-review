# Artificial Personal Assistants

## Definition
AI systems based on Large Language Models designed to assist with personal organization, life planning, and decision-making, with critical implications for capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These tools can create capacity-hostile environments when they encourage users to become plan-followers rather than plan-formers.

## Features and Usage Patterns

### Core Capabilities
Modern APAs provide:
- Life organization and scheduling
- Purpose and value clarification
- Problem reframing and roadblock navigation
- Social validation and interpersonal guidance
- Continuous availability with personalized interaction styles

### Usage Statistics
According to recent studies cited by Ferdman:
- The second and third highest uses of LLMs are for life-planning: "organizing my life" and "finding purpose"
- Users rely on APAs to "help them be more aware of their intentions" and "find small, easy ways of getting started"
- Heavy users utilize LLMs for "social validation, self-regulation, and interpersonal guidance"

## Capacity Impact Analysis

### Plan-Following vs. Plan-Forming
APAs risk transforming users from plan-formers to plan-followers:
- **Plan-forming** requires exercising agential control over settling (deciding between options) and coordinating functions
- **Plan-following** involves responding to triggers in the environment without initiating the planning process

As Ferdman explains: "Relying on an APA to form our plans for us is more like a habit that is developed in response to a trigger in the environment."

### Narrowing of Affordance Space
APAs can create capacity-hostile environments by:
- Reducing opportunities for small-scale decision-making that build broader life-planning capacities
- Discouraging users from seeking alternative avenues for growth and exploration
- Diminishing the development of practical wisdom through reduced real-world experience

### Embodiment and Intersubjectivity Reduction
APAs decrease opportunities for embodied, intersubjective interaction essential for capacity development:
- "AI mediation might come at the expense of human interaction, decreasing the opportunity to develop our capacities in concert with other humans"
- Reduced shared valuing of capacities as users interact primarily with non-human entities

## Market Research Implications

### Researcher Development
APAs used by researchers may:
- Undermine development of practical wisdom needed for complex research design
- Reduce opportunities for embodied interaction essential to qualitative research
- Diminish the habituation process through which researchers develop judgment

### Client Interactions
AI-powered client communication tools risk:
- Creating disembodied interactions that limit understanding of client needs
- Reducing opportunities for researchers to develop social capacities through direct client engagement
- Encouraging plan-following behaviors in research execution

## Mitigation Strategies

### Design Principles
Research organizations should adopt APAs that:
- Preserve researcher agential control over initiating research activities
- Maintain opportunities for embodied interaction with clients and colleagues
- Support rather than replace the mentor-mentee relationship

### Usage Guidelines
Effective APA integration:
- Limits APA use to administrative tasks rather than core research planning
- Requires researchers to articulate their own reasoning before consulting AI
- Creates structured reflection opportunities after AI-assisted work

### Tool Selection Criteria
When evaluating APAs, research organizations should consider:
- Whether the tool encourages plan-forming rather than plan-following
- How it supports embodied interaction opportunities
- Its impact on the habituation process for key research capacities
