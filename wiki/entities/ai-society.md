# AI & Society

First observed: 2026-07-16 | Last updated: 2026-07-18

## Overview
AI & Society is an academic journal published by Springer that focuses on the social, ethical, and philosophical implications of artificial intelligence and digital technologies.

## Key Details
- **Publisher**: Springer
- **Scope**: Interdisciplinary research on AI's societal impacts
- **Notable publications**: Includes [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], which introduced the structural perspective on AI deskilling
- **Reputation**: Recognized as a leading venue for critical analysis of AI's impact on human capacities and social structures

## Relevance to Market Research
The journal publishes research highly relevant to the market research industry, particularly on:
- Ethical implications of AI in data collection and analysis
- Social impacts of AI-driven consumer insights
- Philosophical frameworks for understanding human-AI collaboration in research contexts
- Structural analyses of how AI transforms professional capacities
