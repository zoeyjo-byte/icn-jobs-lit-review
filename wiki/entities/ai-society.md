# AI & SOCIETY

**Type:** Academic Journal
**Publisher:** Springer
**Focus:** Interdisciplinary research on the social, ethical, and philosophical implications of artificial intelligence and digital technologies.

## Overview

AI & SOCIETY is a peer-reviewed academic journal that publishes research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, philosophy, ethics, and social sciences. The journal provides a platform for critical examination of how AI technologies impact human society, values, and flourishing.

## Relevance to Market Research

The journal has become increasingly important for market research professionals seeking to understand the broader societal implications of AI adoption. Articles in AI & SOCIETY often address issues directly relevant to market researchers, including:

- Ethical considerations in AI-driven data collection and analysis
- The impact of AI on human capacities relevant to research (critical thinking, ethical judgment)
- Structural analyses of how AI reshapes professional practices
- Philosophical frameworks for evaluating AI applications

## Notable Publications

- [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]: Avigail Ferdman's 2026 paper introducing the concept of capacity-hostile environments and analyzing AI personal assistants as potential threats to human capacity development.
- Multiple articles examining the ethical implications of AI in data collection and analysis

## Impact on Industry Thinking

AI & SOCIETY has influenced market research thinking by:
- Providing philosophical grounding for concerns about AI deskilling
- Offering frameworks to evaluate AI tools beyond mere efficiency metrics
- Highlighting the structural nature of AI's impact on professional capacities
- Contributing to the development of the [[human-operating-model-gap]] concept
