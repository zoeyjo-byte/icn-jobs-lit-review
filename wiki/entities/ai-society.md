# [[ai-society]]

## Overview
AI & Society is an academic journal published by Springer focusing on the social, ethical, and philosophical implications of artificial intelligence and digital technologies, where [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]] was published. The journal provides a venue for critical reflection on AI's societal impacts.

## Journal Scope
AI & Society publishes research examining:
- Ethical dimensions of AI development and deployment
- Social implications of AI technologies
- Philosophical analyses of AI's impact on human agency and flourishing
- Policy considerations for responsible AI governance
- Critical perspectives on AI's role in society

## Significance of Ferdman Publication
The publication of "AI deskilling is a structural problem" in AI & Society (2026) 41:3001-3013 represents a significant contribution to the field because:

- It introduces the critical distinction between [[capacity-hostile-environments]] and [[capacity-conducive-environments]]
- It provides a structural analysis of how AI systems can undermine human capacities through environmental conditions rather than individual failings
- It develops a philosophical framework connecting AI impacts to human flourishing through the lens of Developmental Perfectionism
- It offers concrete analysis of how [[artificial-personal-assistants]] can create capacity-hostile environments
