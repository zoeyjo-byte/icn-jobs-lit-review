# [[ai-society]]

## Overview

Academic journal published by Springer focusing on the social, ethical, and philosophical implications of artificial intelligence and digital technologies, where [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]] was published.

## Journal Scope

AI & Society is a peer-reviewed academic journal that publishes research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, society, and ethics. The journal specifically focuses on:

- Social implications of AI development and deployment
- Ethical frameworks for AI governance
- Philosophical analyses of AI's impact on human capacities
- Policy considerations for responsible AI adoption
- Interdisciplinary approaches to understanding AI-society relationships

## Notable Publication

The journal published Avigail Ferdman's landmark paper "AI deskilling is a structural problem" in 2026 (Volume 41, pages 3001-3013), which introduced the critical distinction between capacity-hostile and capacity-conducive environments. This paper has become foundational for understanding AI's structural impact on human capacities beyond individual responsibility frameworks.

As described in the paper:

> "Many artificial intelligence tools replace or stand to replace human activity, via automated decision-making, recommender systems and content generation. The more artificial intelligence (AI) replaces valuable human activity, the more it risks deskilling humans of their human capacities. This paper argues for applying a structural perspective to this phenomenon."

## Relevance to Market Research

AI & Society provides critical theoretical frameworks relevant to market research professionals navigating AI adoption:

- Structural analysis of AI's impact on human capacities
- Philosophical foundations for evaluating AI tools beyond efficiency metrics
- Conceptual tools for understanding capacity development pathways
- Ethical considerations for AI implementation in research workflows

The journal's interdisciplinary approach bridges philosophy, social science, and technology studies, offering market researchers valuable perspectives on maintaining human expertise in AI-augmented environments.
