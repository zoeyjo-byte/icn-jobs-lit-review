# AI & Society

## Overview

Academic journal published by Springer focusing on the social, ethical, and philosophical implications of artificial intelligence and digital technologies, where [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]] was published. Provides a venue for critical reflection on AI's societal impacts beyond technical considerations.

## Scope and Focus

The journal serves as an important interdisciplinary platform for:
- Philosophical examinations of AI's impact on human capacities
- Ethical analyses of AI systems in social contexts
- Critical studies of AI's effects on work, relationships, and flourishing
- Structural analyses of how AI reshapes social and material environments
- Interdisciplinary research at the intersection of AI and society
