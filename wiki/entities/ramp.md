# Ramp

## Overview
Ramp is a financial operations platform that processes corporate card and bill pay transactions. It provides the AI Index for measuring AI adoption intensity through corporate spending data.

## Role in AI Research
Ramp's data was central to the [[ramp-revelio-2026-ai-jobs-impact-study]], which linked Ramp's line-item spending data with Revelio Labs' workforce records to study AI's impact on employment.

## AI Index Methodology
- Identifies AI spend from line items using vendor and line-item classification
- Captures spending on foundational large language models, GPU cloud, model serving and inference, coding agents, API tokens, AI image and video generators, and AI search and research software
- Defines AI adoption as the first month of a three-consecutive-month spell where AI spend is at least $100 monthly
- Measures adoption intensity as monthly AI spend per employee (PEPM) over first three months after adoption

## Data Collection
- Observes cleared spend at the line-item level, including date, amount, merchant, firm identifiers, and line-item detail
- Aggregates records to the firm-month level
- Requires firms to have at least six consecutive months of total Ramp spend of $5,000+ to qualify for analysis

## Significance
Ramp's data provides a direct measure of AI adoption through actual spending, overcoming limitations of occupational exposure scores and surveys. This revealed-adoption measure captures firms making sustained, organization-level AI purchases rather than one-off employee experiments.
