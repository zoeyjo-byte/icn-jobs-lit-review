# Zapier

Zapier is a no-code workflow automation platform that connects web applications to automate repetitive tasks across software ecosystems. Founded in 2011, Zapier enables users to create multi-step "Zaps" that trigger actions between apps (e.g., saving email attachments to cloud storage or posting social media updates).

## Relevance to Market Research
Zapier developed the **AI Fluency Rubric** featured in the [[ama-2026-state-of-marketing-careers-report]], providing a framework for assessing organizational AI maturity. The rubric evaluates four dimensions: prompt engineering proficiency, AI output validation, ethical implementation, and strategic integration. This tool helps market research teams measure their capacity to leverage AI while preserving critical human skills like [[epistemic-skills]] and [[social-skills]].

## Case Study Application
In the AMA report, Zapier's rubric demonstrated how organizations transition from basic AI tool usage (Level 1) to strategic AI orchestration (Level 4). Research teams at Level 4 use AI for complex insight synthesis while maintaining human oversight for contextual interpretation—directly addressing the [[human-operating-model-gap]]. The case study highlights Zapier's own implementation where researchers reduced data processing time by 65% while increasing strategic analysis capacity.
