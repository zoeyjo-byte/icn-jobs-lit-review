## Revelio Labs

**First Observed:** 2026-07-03
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-03

### Overview
Revelio Labs is a workforce intelligence platform that aggregates data from online professional profiles to construct longitudinal employment histories. By analyzing millions of publicly available career records, Revelio provides granular insights into workforce composition, movement, and evolution across industries and occupations.

### Data Collection Methodology
- **Source Data**: Publicly available online professional profiles (LinkedIn, company sites, etc.)
- **Aggregation**: Constructs longitudinal employment histories for individual workers
- **Fields Captured**: Employers, job titles, employment dates, locations, seniority levels
- **Weighting**: Adjusts for differential platform usage to improve labor market representativeness

### Seniority Classification System
Revelio's seven-point seniority field enables precise workforce segmentation:
- **Entry-Level**: Seniority 1-2 (typically 0-3 years experience)
- **Non-Entry**: Seniority 3-7
- **Manager-Plus**: Seniority 4-7
- **Engineering Roles**: Further segmented by technical specialization

### Key Applications in AI Research
Revelio data enabled the Ramp-Revelio 2026 study to:
- Track monthly employment changes at 21,559 firms
- Analyze role-specific impacts of AI adoption
- Measure entry-level vs. senior workforce composition shifts
- Validate findings against traditional labor statistics through RPLS

### Validation and Reliability
- **Revelio Public Labor Statistics (RPLS)**: Benchmarked against official statistics
- **Weighting Methodology**: Corrects for platform usage differences across demographics
- **Academic Adoption**: Used in numerous peer-reviewed studies on workforce dynamics
- **Limitations**: Coverage varies by occupation, industry, and geography

### Key Findings Enabled by Revelio Data
- High-intensity AI adopters increase entry-level headcount by 12.0%
- Employment gains are broad across roles (engineering, sales, admin, customer service)
- Sectoral differences in AI impact are concentrated in Information firms
- Pre-adoption growth trajectories differ significantly between adopters and non-adopters

### Relevance to Market Research
Revelio's approach offers market researchers:
- Granular workforce composition data beyond traditional surveys
- Longitudinal tracking of career progression and role evolution
- Objective measurement of talent market dynamics
- Framework for analyzing how AI reshapes specific job functions
