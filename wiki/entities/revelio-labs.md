# Revelio Labs

## Overview
Revelio Labs is a workforce intelligence platform that aggregates data from online professional profiles to construct longitudinal employment histories. It provides seniority classification and workforce composition data.

## Role in AI Research
Revelio Labs' workforce data was central to the [[ramp-revelio-2026-ai-jobs-impact-study]], which linked Revelio's employment records with Ramp's AI spending data to study AI's impact on employment.

## Data Collection Methodology
- Aggregates information from millions of publicly available online professional profiles
- Constructs longitudinal employment histories for individual workers
- Each profile contains information on employers, job titles, employment dates, locations, and other career characteristics
- Uses a seven-point seniority field to define:
  - Entry-level headcount: seniority 1-2
  - Non-entry headcount: seniority 3-7
  - Manager-plus headcount: seniority 4-7
- Applies weighting methodology to adjust for differential platform usage and improve representativeness

## Data Validation
- Prior validation exercises show weighted Revelio measures closely track benchmark labor market statistics
- Similar worker-level employment histories have been used extensively in academic research

## Significance
Revelio Labs provides granular workforce data that enables analysis of not just total firm employment but also hiring, attrition, occupational composition, worker seniority, and other workforce characteristics difficult to observe in traditional data sources.
