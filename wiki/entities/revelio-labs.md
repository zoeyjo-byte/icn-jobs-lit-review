# Revelio Labs

Revelio Labs is a workforce intelligence platform that aggregates information from millions of publicly available online professional profiles to construct longitudinal employment histories for individual workers.

## Data Collection Methodology

Revelio Labs collects data from:
- Publicly available online professional profiles
- Employment histories including job titles, O*NET codes, seniority, and estimated salaries
- Monthly employment records at the firm level

The platform applies a weighting methodology that adjusts for differential platform usage to improve representativeness across the labor market. Prior validation exercises have shown that weighted Revelio measures closely track benchmark labor market statistics.

## Role in AI Research

Revelio Labs' workforce data has been instrumental in studies examining AI's impact on employment, most notably the [[ramp-revelio-2026-ai-jobs-impact-study]]. By linking Ramp's AI spending data to Revelio's workforce records, researchers could directly observe how AI adoption affects:

- Total employment
- Entry-level headcount
- Role-specific employment (engineering, sales, administration, etc.)
- Workforce composition by education level

## Seniority Classification

A key feature of Revelio's data is its seven-point seniority field, which allows for detailed analysis of workforce composition:
- Entry-level headcount: seniority 1-2
- Non-entry headcount: seniority 3-7
- Manager-plus headcount: seniority 4-7

This granularity enabled the Ramp-Revelio study to demonstrate that high-intensity AI adopters increased entry-level headcount by 12.0%, countering predictions that AI would reduce junior employment.

## Relevance to Market Research

For market research professionals, Revelio Labs provides valuable insights into workforce trends and employment patterns. The platform's ability to track changes in occupational composition, worker seniority, and other workforce characteristics offers a rich data source for understanding labor market dynamics.
