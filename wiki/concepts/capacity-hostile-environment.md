# [[capacity-hostile-environment]]

## Definition
Environmental conditions that systematically impede development/exercise of human capacities through:
- Narrowed affordance fields
- Reduced embodied interaction
- Automation of critical thinking tasks

## Key Characteristics
- Discourages **agential control** over activity initiation
- Limits opportunities for **habituation** through practice
- Undermines intersubjective valuing of capacities

## Examples
- Social media platforms
- Over-reliant APA systems
- Healthcare AI decision tools
