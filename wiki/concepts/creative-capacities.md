# creative-capacities

## Definition
Creative capacities are the human capacities related to generating novel ideas, making unfamiliar combinations of familiar concepts, and transforming conceptual spaces, which enable innovation and adaptation.

## Key Insights
According to Ferdman's 2026 analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:
- Creative capacities involve making unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas
- They entail transforming conceptual spaces without relying merely on luck, accidents, or mechanical procedures
- Creativity is necessary for responding to novel situations, surprises, and unexpected events
- The capacity for creativity is essential for innovation and adaptation in changing circumstances
- Creative capacities are part of the core human capacities that constitute flourishing

## Connection to AI Systems
AI systems can undermine creative capacities by:
- Providing pre-determined solutions that reduce opportunities for novel idea generation
- Creating environments with narrow fields of affordances that limit creative exploration
- Automating tasks that would otherwise require creative problem-solving
- Encouraging plan-following rather than the creative adaptation required in plan-forming
- Reducing opportunities for embodied interaction that often sparks creative insights

## Implications for Market Research
In market research, creative capacities are essential for:
- Developing innovative research methodologies
- Interpreting unexpected findings in novel ways
- Creating compelling narratives from research data
- Adapting research approaches to unique client needs
- Transforming conceptual spaces to generate new market insights
- Responding creatively to research challenges and surprises

## Mitigation Strategies
To protect creative capacities in AI-assisted market research:
- Design AI tools that support rather than replace creative exploration
- Create research environments that encourage novel combinations of ideas
- Maintain opportunities for researchers to transform conceptual spaces
- Implement training that emphasizes creative problem-solving
- Ensure AI implementation doesn't automate the tasks that require creative adaptation
- Foster spaces for embodied interaction that often spark creative insights
