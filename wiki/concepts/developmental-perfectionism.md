# [[developmental-perfectionism]]

## Definition
Developmental Perfectionism is a neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life, providing the philosophical foundation for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful. As established in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this framework explains why the impoverishment of human capacities diminishes us as humans.

## Core Principles
Ferdman's analysis draws on Developmental Perfectionism to argue that:

1. **Flourishing as Unified Life**: Human flourishing is "a complex, unified, and well-rounded life" (Hurka 1993) rather than the accumulation of isolated goods

2. **Intrinsic Value of Capacities**: The competent exercise of human capacities is the manifestation of intrinsically valuable goods, making the capacities themselves intrinsically valuable

3. **Constitutive Relationship**: Human capacities are constitutive of flourishing—"the value of being on the summit of Mt. Everest is gained from the competent exercise of a combination of capacities involved in climbing it, and not from simply being there"

4. **Developmental Process**: Flourishing involves the development and exercise of capacities through a process of habituation rather than static achievement

## Human Capacities Framework
Developmental Perfectionism identifies four core categories of human capacities:

1. **Epistemic Capacities**: Including theoretical rationality (thinking, considering reasons, forming beliefs) and practical rationality (phronesis or wisdom)

2. **Social Capacities**: Including moral capacity and social/caring capacities for developing meaningful relationships

3. **Creative Capacities**: Involving making unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas and transforming conceptual spaces

4. **Volitional Capacity**: The meta-capacity to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow to achieve intrinsically valuable goals

## Implications for AI Deskilling
Ferdman uses Developmental Perfectionism to explain why AI-induced capacity deskilling is harmful:

- **Capacity Impoverishment**: "Impoverished capacities (i.e., capacity-deskilling), therefore, lead to impoverished lives"
- **Structural Harm**: Deskilling is not merely an individual failing but a structural problem that undermines the conditions for human flourishing
- **Beyond Instrumental Value**: Human capacities have intrinsic value beyond their instrumental utility

## Contrast with Other Approaches
Developmental Perfectionism differs from:

- **Utilitarian Approaches**: Which focus on maximizing outcomes rather than the development of capacities
- **Individual Responsibility Models**: Which frame deskilling as a personal failing rather than a structural issue
- **Purely Instrumental Views of AI**: Which evaluate AI solely on productivity metrics without considering capacity impacts

## Implications for Market Research
For the market research industry, Developmental Perfectionism suggests:

- Evaluation frameworks should measure capacity development alongside productivity
- Research organizations should design environments that support the development of all four capacity types
- The [[human-operating-model-gap]] reflects a mismatch between AI capabilities and human flourishing requirements
- The [[value-chain-shift]] should be evaluated based on its impact on human capacity development
