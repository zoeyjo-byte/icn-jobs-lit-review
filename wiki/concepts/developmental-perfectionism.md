# Developmental Perfectionism

**First Observed**: 2026-07-15 | **Last Updated**: 2026-07-22

Developmental Perfectionism is a neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life, providing the philosophical foundation for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful. As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this framework explains why the competent exercise of human capacities constitutes intrinsic value rather than merely instrumental value.

## Core Principles

### Unified Flourishing
- Human flourishing is not merely accumulation of goods but a complex, integrated state
- Capacities develop synergistically; flourishing requires balanced development across domains
- Example: Epistemic capacities support social capacities, which in turn support creative capacities

### Constitutive Value
- The competent exercise of capacities yields outcomes with intrinsic value (knowledge, beauty, morality, friendship)
- Capacities are intrinsically valuable because they constitute the achievement of these goods
- Example: The value of being on Mt. Everest comes from competent exercise of climbing capacities, not merely being there

### Developmental Process
- Flourishing involves ongoing growth rather than static achievement
- Capacity development requires effort, overcoming difficulty, and perseverance
- Example: Developing critical thinking involves encountering and overcoming intellectual challenges

## Four Core Human Capacities

### Epistemic Capacities
- Theoretical rationality: thinking, considering reasons, forming beliefs, contemplating
- Practical rationality (phronesis): doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason
- Essential for navigating truth and making informed decisions

### Social Capacities
- Moral capacity: recognizing others as moral agents, understanding ethical principles
- Relational capacity: forming meaningful relationships of friendship and love
- Critical for participating in communal life and shared valuing

### Creative Capacities
- Making unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas
- Transforming conceptual spaces without relying on luck or mechanical procedures
- Enables innovation and adaptation to novel situations

### Volitional Capacity
- Ability to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow
- Required to achieve intrinsically valuable goals
- Functions as a meta-capacity necessary for developing all other capacities

## Implications for AI Deskilling
Developmental Perfectionism explains why capacity-deskilling is harmful:

### Constitutive Harm
- Capacity-deskilling is bad because capacities are constitutive of flourishing
- Impoverished capacities lead to impoverished lives
- Example: Deskilling of critical thinking capacity diminishes one's ability to navigate truth

### Structural Perspective
- Deskilling should be understood as structural problem, not individual failing
- AI creates environments hostile to capacity cultivation
- Example: AI systems that automate decision-making create narrow fields of affordances

### Evaluation Framework
- AI applications should be evaluated based on conduciveness to capacity cultivation
- Systems that encourage plan-forming rather than plan-following support flourishing
- Example: Tools that scaffold critical thinking rather than replace it are capacity-conducive

## Contrast with Instrumentalist Views
Unlike instrumentalist approaches that treat capacities as tools to achieve goals, Developmental Perfectionism recognizes:
- The process of capacity development has intrinsic value
- Being a certain kind of person matters more than merely achieving outcomes
- Example: Life-planning has value in the self-constituting activity, not just in achieving planned goals

## Practical Applications
This framework informs:
- AI design principles that preserve opportunities for capacity development
- Educational approaches that balance AI assistance with meaningful practice
- Workplace policies that maintain embodied, intersubjective learning opportunities
- Public policy supporting capacity-conducive environments through community infrastructure
