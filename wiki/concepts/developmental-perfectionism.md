## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Developmental Perfectionism is a neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life. Humans exercise their capacities in activities that yield outcomes with intrinsic value like knowledge, beauty, morality, and friendship.

Key principles:
- The competent exercise of human capacities is the manifestation of intrinsically valuable goods
- The value of an achievement is gained from the competent exercise of capacities involved
- Capacity-deskilling leads to impoverished lives because human capacities are constitutive of flourishing

Capacity connections:
- Epistemic capacities enable knowledge acquisition and practical wisdom
- Social capacities enable meaningful relationships and moral understanding
- Creative capacities enable innovation and adaptation
- Volitional capacity enables the development of all other capacities

Market research implications:
- Research should be framed as an intrinsically valuable activity that contributes to human flourishing
- AI tools should be evaluated based on whether they support or undermine the competent exercise of research capacities
- Research firms should design work that allows researchers to experience the intrinsic value of their work
- Career development should focus on building well-rounded researchers rather than just technical specialists
