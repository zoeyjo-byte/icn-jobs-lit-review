# [[developmental-perfectionism]]

## Definition
Neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life, providing the philosophical foundation for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful. As established in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this framework explains why impoverished capacities lead to impoverished lives.

## Core Principles
- Human flourishing involves the competent exercise of core capacities
- Capacities are intrinsically valuable as manifestations of intrinsically valuable goods
- Flourishing is a unified achievement rather than collection of separate goods
- The value of activities comes from competent capacity exercise, not just outcomes
- Capacity development requires effort, growth, and overcoming difficulty

## Connection to Human Capacities
- Epistemic capacities yield knowledge as an intrinsically valuable good
- Social capacities yield meaningful relationships and moral understanding
- Creative capacities yield transformed conceptual spaces and novel ideas
- Volitional capacity enables development of all other capacities
- Physical capacities (not discussed in paper) complete the flourishing picture

## Market Research Implications
- AI tools that undermine capacity development ultimately diminish research quality
- Research organizations should evaluate AI implementations based on flourishing criteria
- Capacity-deskilling in research leads to impoverished insights and understanding
- Research excellence requires well-rounded development of researcher capacities
- The value of research comes from competent capacity exercise, not just reports

## AI Evaluation Criteria
- Does the tool preserve opportunities for agential control?
- Does it support gradual habituation through practice?
- Does it encourage embodied, intersubjective interaction?
- Does it maintain opportunities for plan-forming rather than plan-following?
- Does it support the development of practical wisdom in researchers?
