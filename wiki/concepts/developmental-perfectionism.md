# [[developmental-perfectionism]]

## Definition

Neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life, providing the philosophical foundation for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful, as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Philosophical Foundation

Ferdman explains Developmental Perfectionism as:

> "A neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing (Kauppinen 2025), where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life (Hurka 1993). Humans exercise their capacities in activities that yield outcomes that have intrinsic value like knowledge, beauty, morality, friendship (Kraut 2013; Bradford 2016, 2021; Fletcher 2016). The competent exercise of the human capacities is the manifestation of the achievement of the intrinsically valuable goods, making the capacities intrinsically valuable too."

This framework provides the normative basis for why capacity deskilling matters:

> "Developmental Perfectionism neatly captures why deskilling is bad: capacity-deskilling is bad because our human capacities are constitutive of our flourishing. Impoverished capacities (i.e., capacity-deskilling), therefore, lead to impoverished lives (Ferdman 2024)."

## Core Human Capacities

The framework identifies four categories of core human capacities essential for flourishing:

### 1. Epistemic Capacities
- Theoretical rationality: thinking, considering reasons to believe, forming beliefs, contemplating
- Practical rationality: manifesting in phronesis (common sense or wisdom), doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason

### 2. Social Capacities
- Moral capacity
- Social and caring capacities for developing meaningful relationships of friendship and love

### 3. Creative Capacity
- Making unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas
- Transforming conceptual spaces without relying merely on luck or mechanical procedures

### 4. Volitional Capacity (Meta-Capacity)
- The ability to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere and grow
- Required for developing and exercising all other capacities

As Ferdman notes, these capacities rarely operate in isolation: "the capacity to know involves theoretical rationality as well as practical rationality, the capacity to will ourselves to acquire knowledge, and the physical capacities, insofar as knowledge is embodied."

## Contrast with Skill Obsolescence

Developmental Perfectionism distinguishes between benign skill obsolescence and harmful capacity deskilling:

> "Generally, a skill is a kind of disposition to form knowledge appropriate to a particular domain of human activity... Yet 'skill' is sometimes used to denote expertise in domains, such as hunting/gathering, basket-weaving, writing, coding, navigating, and composing music. On this use, deskilling is not always negative: skill obsolescence has always been part of human history, where technological developments free up time and resources for the cultivation of other skills... The deskilling of our core human capacities—to know, create, form meaningful relationships, and use our willpower—is a different matter."

While basket-weaving may become obsolete without harm, deskilling of core human capacities diminishes us as humans.

## Implications for AI Ethics

The framework provides critical guidance for evaluating AI systems:

> "Rather than focusing only on how human agents ought to engage with AI tools, we should also ask what kind of socio-technical environments would be best for the cultivation of our personhood... As such, we have reason to ensure that socio-technical environments we inhabit are conducive, rather than hostile, to the cultivation of our human capacities."

This shifts the ethical burden from individual responsibility to structural design:

> "Framing deskilling as a matter of individual responsibility—expecting people to simply will themselves to cultivate 'the arts of personhood'—overlooks crucial structural aspects of the problem. To a large extent, we develop and exercise our epistemic, moral, social, and creative capacities in response to our social and material environment."

## Market Research Applications

In market research contexts, Developmental Perfectionism helps evaluate:

- Whether AI tools preserve opportunities for developing **practical wisdom** in research design
- If automated analysis systems undermine the **creative capacity** needed for insight generation
- How remote research methods affect the **social capacities** required for client relationships
- Whether workflow automation diminishes the **volitional capacity** needed to overcome research challenges

The framework suggests that AI implementations should be evaluated not just on efficiency but on their contribution to researcher flourishing through capacity development.
