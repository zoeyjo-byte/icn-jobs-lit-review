# [[developmental-perfectionism]]

## Definition
Neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life, providing the philosophical foundation for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful, as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Key Principles
- **Complex unity**: Human flourishing is a complex, unified, and well-rounded life
- **Intrinsic value**: Activities yield outcomes with intrinsic value (knowledge, beauty, morality, friendship)
- **Capacity manifestation**: Competent exercise of human capacities manifests achievement of intrinsically valuable goods
- **Constitutive value**: Human capacities are intrinsically valuable as they are constitutive of flourishing
- **Developmental process**: Flourishing involves a developmental process of capacity cultivation

## Core Human Capacities
Developmental Perfectionism identifies four core capacity domains:
- [[epistemic-capacities]]: Related to knowledge, reasoning, and understanding
- [[social-capacities]]: Related to forming meaningful relationships
- [[creative-capacities]]: Related to generating novel ideas
- [[volitional-capacity]]: Meta-capacity to exert effort and overcome difficulty

## Implications for AI Impact
As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this framework explains why:
- Capacity-deskilling is harmful (impoverished capacities lead to impoverished lives)
- Deskilling should be understood as a structural problem
- Environments should be evaluated based on conduciveness to capacity cultivation
- AI systems that undermine capacity development threaten human flourishing

## Application to Market Research
This framework helps understand:
- Why the [[entry-level-squeeze]] phenomenon is problematic (reduces capacity development opportunities)
- How the [[human-operating-model-gap]] undermines professional flourishing
- Why capacity-conducive environments are essential for quality insights work
- How AI adoption should be evaluated based on capacity cultivation impact

## Contrast with Instrumentalist Views
Developmental Perfectionism differs from instrumentalist views by:
- Understanding life-planning as self-constituting rather than merely goal-achieving
- Recognizing that the competent exercise of capacities has intrinsic value
- Emphasizing the developmental process rather than just outcomes
- Viewing capacities as constitutive of flourishing rather than merely instrumental
