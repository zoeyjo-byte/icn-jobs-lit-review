# [[developmental-perfectionism]]

## Definition
Neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life, providing the philosophical foundation for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful. As detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this framework explains why impoverished capacities lead to impoverished lives.

## Core Principles
- Human flourishing involves competent exercise of core capacities
- Capacities are intrinsically valuable as manifestations of achievement
- Flourishing is a unified, complex achievement rather than isolated goods
- Development of capacities requires effort, growth, and overcoming difficulty
- Impoverished capacities lead to impoverished lives

## Capacity-Flourishing Relationship
Developmental Perfectionism establishes that:
- Humans exercise capacities in activities yielding intrinsically valuable outcomes
- Competent capacity exercise manifests achievement of intrinsically valuable goods
- Capacities are constitutive of flourishing, not merely instrumental
- The value of outcomes (e.g., summiting Everest) comes from competent exercise of capacities
- Capacity-deskilling diminishes us as humans by undermining flourishing

## Core Human Capacities
The framework identifies four core capacities constitutive of flourishing:

### Epistemic Capacities
- Theoretical rationality: thinking, considering reasons, forming beliefs
- Practical rationality (phronesis): doing right thing in right way for right reason

### Social Capacities
- Moral capacity
- Capacities for developing meaningful relationships of friendship and love

### Creative Capacities
- Making unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas
- Transforming conceptual spaces without relying on luck

### Volitional Capacity
- Meta-capacity to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere
- Required for developing all other capacities
- Enables growth toward intrinsically valuable goals

## AI Deskilling Implications
From this perspective:
- Capacity-deskilling is bad because capacities are constitutive of flourishing
- AI systems that undermine capacity development diminish human flourishing
- Deskilling of core human capacities (vs. domain-specific skills) is particularly concerning
- Structural solutions are needed to preserve capacity-conducive environments

## Market Research Applications
- Research organizations should evaluate AI implementations based on capacity preservation
- Training programs should emphasize development of all four capacity types
- Career paths should provide opportunities for balanced capacity development
- Industry standards should protect against capacity-hostile research environments
- Leadership should foster environments supporting volitional capacity growth
