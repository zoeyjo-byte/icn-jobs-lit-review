# Developmental Perfectionism

**First Observed:** 2026-07-15
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-16

## Definition

Developmental Perfectionism is a neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life. It provides the philosophical foundation for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful to human beings.

## Key Principles

According to Ferdman's 2026 analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

- Human flourishing involves the **competent exercise of core human capacities**
- These capacities yield outcomes with **intrinsic value** like knowledge, beauty, morality, friendship
- The competent exercise of capacities is the **manifestation of intrinsically valuable goods**
- Capacities are **intrinsically valuable** because they constitute flourishing
- Flourishing is a **complex, unified, and well-rounded life** (Hurka 1993)

## Core Human Capacities

Developmental Perfectionism identifies four key capacity domains:

### 1. Epistemic Capacities
- **Theoretical rationality**: Thinking, considering reasons, forming beliefs, contemplating
- **Practical rationality**: Manifesting in phronesis (common sense or wisdom)

### 2. Social Capacities
- **Moral capacity**: Developing ethical judgment and action
- **Social and caring capacities**: For meaningful relationships of friendship and love

### 3. Creative Capacity
- Making **unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas**
- Transforming conceptual spaces without relying on luck or mechanical procedures

### 4. Volitional Capacity
- The **meta-capacity** to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere and grow
- Required for developing and exercising all other capacities

## Why Deskilling is Bad

Developmental Perfectionism explains why deskilling is harmful:
- Capacity-deskilling leads to **impoverished lives**
- Inadequate development of capacities **diminishes us as humans**
- Impoverished capacities undermine our ability to achieve **intrinsically valuable goods**
- Deskilling of core human capacities is bad because they are **constitutive of flourishing**
