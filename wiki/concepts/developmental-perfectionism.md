# Developmental Perfectionism

Developmental Perfectionism is a neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life (Hurka 1993). According to this framework, humans exercise their capacities in activities that yield outcomes with intrinsic value like knowledge, beauty, morality, and friendship (Kraut 2013; Bradford 2016, 2021; Fletcher 2016). The competent exercise of human capacities is the manifestation of achieving intrinsically valuable goods, making the capacities themselves intrinsically valuable.

This philosophical framework is critical for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful. As Avigail Ferdman argues in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], capacity-deskilling is bad because human capacities are constitutive of our flourishing. Impoverished capacities lead to impoverished lives. For example, the value of being on the summit of a mountain is gained from the competent exercise of climbing capacities, not from simply being there (say, if one arrives there by helicopter).

Developmental Perfectionism provides the normative foundation for evaluating AI applications based on their conduciveness or hostility to capacity cultivation. It shifts the focus from individual responsibility for cultivating capacities to the societal obligation to foster environments that support human flourishing.

## Key Components

1. **Intrinsically Valuable Goods**: Activities that yield knowledge, beauty, morality, and friendship have intrinsic value.

2. **Competent Exercise**: The value comes from competently exercising capacities, not merely achieving outcomes.

3. **Unified Life**: Flourishing involves a complex, well-rounded integration of multiple capacities rather than excelling in isolated domains.

## Implications for Market Research

In the market research industry, Developmental Perfectionism suggests that AI tools should be evaluated not just on efficiency metrics but on how they support or undermine researchers' capacity to engage meaningfully with data, clients, and research subjects. Tools that automate the entire research process without opportunities for researchers to exercise judgment risk creating capacity-hostile environments.
