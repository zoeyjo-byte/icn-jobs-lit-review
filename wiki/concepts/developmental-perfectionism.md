# Developmental Perfectionism

## Definition
A **neo-Aristotelian approach to human flourishing** where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life, providing the philosophical foundation for understanding why capacity-deskilling is harmful, as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Philosophical Foundation
Developmental Perfectionism, as articulated by Kauppinen (2025) and others:
- Views human flourishing as the competent exercise of core human capacities
- Understands capacities as intrinsically valuable because they manifest intrinsically valuable goods
- Recognizes that the value of outcomes (like reaching a mountain summit) comes from the competent exercise of capacities involved
- Emphasizes that flourishing is a developmental process rather than a static state

## Core Argument
Capacity-deskilling is bad because:
- Human capacities are **constitutive of flourishing**
- Inadequate development of capacities leads to "capacity impoverishment"
- Capacity impoverishment diminishes us as humans and undermines flourishing
- Therefore, we have reason to ensure environments are conducive to capacity cultivation

## Connection to AI Deskilling
This framework explains why AI-induced deskilling is particularly concerning:
- Unlike deskilling of specific activities (hunting, coding), deskilling of core human capacities undermines our very humanity
- AI systems that create **capacity-hostile environments** prevent the development of capacities constitutive of flourishing
- The problem requires structural solutions because flourishing depends on the right kind of socio-technical environments

## Practical Implications for Market Research
- Organizations should evaluate AI tools based on their conduciveness to capacity development
- Work redesign should focus on preserving opportunities for exercising core capacities
- Training programs should address not just technical skills but the development of capacities
- Leadership should foster environments where capacities can flourish alongside AI adoption
