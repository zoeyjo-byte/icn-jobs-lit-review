# human-capacities

## Definition
Human capacities are core abilities that constitute human flourishing, including epistemic, social, creative, and volitional capacities, which are fundamental to being human and can be undermined by AI systems.

## Key Insights
According to Ferdman's 2026 analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:
- Human capacities are more analogous to virtues than to domain-specific skills
- The deskilling of core human capacities is bad because inadequate development can lead to "capacity impoverishment"
- Capacity impoverishment diminishes us as humans and undermines our flourishing
- Unlike skill obsolescence in domains like hunting or coding, capacity-deskilling should always be a cause for worry
- We have reason to ensure socio-technical environments are conducive to the cultivation of human capacities

## Types of Human Capacities
### Epistemic Capacities
- Theoretical rationality: thinking, considering reasons to believe, forming beliefs, contemplating
- Practical rationality (phronesis/common sense or wisdom): doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason

### Social Capacities
- Moral capacity: the ability to make ethical judgments and act morally
- Social and caring capacities: for developing meaningful relationships of friendship and love

### Creative Capacities
- Making unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas
- Transforming conceptual spaces without relying merely on luck, accidents, or mechanical procedures

### Volitional Capacity
- The ability to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere and grow
- Required to achieve intrinsically valuable goals
- Functions as a meta-capacity necessary for developing all other capacities

## Connection to AI Systems
AI systems can undermine human capacities by:
- Creating environments with narrow fields of affordances that limit capacity exercise
- Reducing opportunities for embodied interaction necessary for social capacity development
- Encouraging plan-following rather than plan-forming, undermining practical rationality
- Automating routine tasks that would otherwise provide opportunities for capacity habituation

## Implications for Market Research
In market research, preserving human capacities is essential for:
- Maintaining researchers' ability to exercise practical wisdom in research design
- Ensuring researchers develop moral capacities for ethical research practice
- Preserving creative capacities for innovative research approaches
- Supporting the volitional capacity necessary to overcome research challenges

## Mitigation Strategies
To protect human capacities in AI-assisted market research:
- Design AI tools that maintain opportunities for exercising all human capacities
- Create research environments that encourage embodied interaction
- Ensure AI implementation doesn't narrow the field of affordances for capacity development
- Develop training programs that emphasize the development of the full range of human capacities
