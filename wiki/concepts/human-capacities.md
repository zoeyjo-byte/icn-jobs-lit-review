# [[human-capacities]]

## Definition
Human capacities are core abilities that constitute human flourishing, including epistemic, social, creative, and volitional capacities, which are fundamental to being human and can be undermined by AI systems. As established in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], these capacities are constitutive of flourishing and their deskilling leads to impoverished lives.

## Four Core Capacities
Ferdman identifies four fundamental human capacities:

### [[epistemic-capacities]]
The capacities related to knowledge, reasoning, and understanding:
- Theoretical rationality: thinking, considering reasons, forming beliefs, contemplating
- Practical rationality (phronesis/common sense/wisdom): doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason

### [[social-capacities]]
The capacities related to forming meaningful relationships:
- Moral capacity: recognizing others as moral agents, making ethical judgments
- Social and caring capacities: developing meaningful relationships of friendship and love

### [[creative-capacities]]
The capacities related to generating novel ideas:
- Making unfamiliar combinations of familiar concepts
- Transforming conceptual spaces without relying on luck or mechanical procedures

### [[volitional-capacity]]
The meta-capacity to exert effort and overcome difficulty:
- The ability to persevere and grow to achieve intrinsically valuable goals
- Required for developing and exercising all other capacities
- Particularly important given that developing capacities is difficult

## Interdependence
These capacities rarely operate in isolation:
- The capacity to know involves theoretical and practical rationality
- Practical wisdom requires epistemic capacities and volitional capacity
- Creative problem-solving draws on multiple capacities simultaneously
- Moral reasoning integrates epistemic, social, and volitional capacities

## Implications for AI Systems
Ferdman argues that AI systems can undermine human capacities through:
- Creating environments with narrow fields of affordances
- Reducing opportunities for embodied interaction
- Disrupting intersubjective learning processes
- Automating routine tasks essential for habituation
- Encouraging plan-following rather than plan-forming

## Professional Context
In market research, these capacities manifest as:
- Epistemic: Research design, data interpretation, critical thinking
- Social: Building rapport with participants, ethical research conduct
- Creative: Innovative research approaches, novel insights
- Volitional: Perseverance through research challenges, overcoming obstacles
