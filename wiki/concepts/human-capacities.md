## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Human capacities are core abilities that constitute human flourishing, including epistemic, social, creative, and volitional capacities. These capacities are fundamental to being human and can be undermined by AI systems.

Core capacities:
- Epistemic capacities: theoretical rationality (thinking, considering reasons) and practical rationality/phronesis (wisdom)
- Social capacities: moral capacity and social/caring capacities for relationships
- Creative capacities: generating novel ideas, transforming conceptual spaces
- Volitional capacity: ability to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere

Importance:
- These capacities are constitutive of human flourishing
- Inadequate development leads to "capacity impoverishment"
- Capacity-deskilling diminishes us as humans

Threats from AI:
- Automation that offloads routine tasks deprives users of habituation opportunities
- Disembodied environments reduce opportunities for social capacity development
- Plan-following tools undermine the development of practical wisdom

Preservation strategies:
- Design work environments that afford opportunities for capacity exercise
- Balance AI automation with human-led activities
- Create mentorship programs that transmit not just skills but their value
- Evaluate AI tools based on their conduciveness to capacity cultivation
