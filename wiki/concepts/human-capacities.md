# [[human-capacities]]

## Definition

Human capacities are core abilities that constitute human flourishing, including epistemic, social, creative, and volitional capacities, which are fundamental to being human and can be undermined by AI systems. As established in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], these capacities are constitutive of our flourishing, and their deskilling leads to impoverished lives.

## Four Core Capacities

### Epistemic Capacities
- **Theoretical rationality**: Thinking, considering reasons to believe, forming beliefs, contemplating
- **Practical rationality (phronesis)**: Doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason
- Essential for knowledge acquisition, critical thinking, and understanding

### Social Capacities
- **Moral capacity**: Recognizing others as moral agents, making ethical judgments
- **Relational capacity**: Forming meaningful relationships of friendship and love
- Essential for communal life and shared valuing

### Creative Capacities
- Making unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas
- Transforming conceptual spaces without relying on luck or mechanical procedures
- Essential for innovation and adaptation

### Volitional Capacity
- The ability to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow
- Required to achieve intrinsically valuable goals
- Functions as a meta-capacity necessary for developing all other capacities

As Ferdman explains: "The competent exercise of the human capacities is the manifestation of the achievement of the intrinsically valuable goods, making the capacities intrinsically valuable too."

## Why Capacities Matter

Human capacities are constitutive of flourishing because:
- They enable engagement with intrinsically valuable goods (knowledge, beauty, morality, friendship)
- Their competent exercise yields outcomes with intrinsic value
- Impoverished capacities lead to impoverished lives
- They are necessary for the "arts of personhood" required for practical reason, self-worth, and mutual respect

Ferdman states: "Developmental Perfectionism neatly captures why deskilling is bad: capacity-deskilling is bad because our human capacities are constitutive of our flourishing."

## Threats from AI Systems

AI systems threaten human capacities by:
- Creating **capacity-hostile environments** that restrict opportunities for capacity development
- Automating routine tasks that provide essential practice opportunities
- Reducing embodied interaction necessary for social and moral capacities
- Encouraging **plan-following** rather than **plan-forming**, undermining agential control
- Creating narrow fields of affordances that discourage capacity exercise

## Implications for Market Research

Human capacities are critical for market research because:
- Epistemic capacities enable nuanced data interpretation
- Social capacities support ethical research practices and client relationships
- Creative capacities drive innovative methodology development
- Volitional capacity sustains researchers through challenging projects

AI tools that automate research processes risk deskilling these capacities when they:
- Replace researcher judgment with algorithmic outputs
- Reduce opportunities for embodied interaction in research settings
- Eliminate routine tasks necessary for habituation of research skills
- Undermine opportunities for creative problem-solving

## Preservation Strategies

To protect human capacities in AI-augmented market research:
- Design AI tools that preserve opportunities for human judgment
- Maintain embodied interaction spaces for research teams
- Ensure researchers experience the full research cycle, including failures
- Create mentorship programs that support intersubjective habituation
- Develop evaluation frameworks that value capacity development
