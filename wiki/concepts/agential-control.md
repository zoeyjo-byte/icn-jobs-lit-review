# Agential Control

## Definition
The ability to **adjust the execution of an action as the performance unfolds**, a key component of being skilled that distinguishes skills from habits, as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. Skills require control over initiating the task, whereas habits are merely triggered by environmental cues.

## Philosophical Foundation
Drawing on skill philosophy, Ferdman explains that:
- To be skilled is to have **flexible links between thought and action** that continue to evolve even after expertise is achieved
- **Agency development** involves a shift in the balance between automaticity and control through tuning and attuning
- Skills differ from habits because they require **control over initiating the activity**, not just responding to environmental triggers

## Critical Distinction: Plan-Forming vs Plan-Following
This concept is central to understanding AI's impact:
- **Plan-forming**: Requires agential control to initiate and adjust plans through self-deliberation
- **Plan-following**: Involves responding to triggers in the environment (like AI recommendations)

As Ferdman states: "The implication is that an agent who performs out of habit, in response to triggers in the environment, is less skilled compared to the agent who initiated the activity, who has full agential control over it."

## Impact on Market Research Roles
AI systems that remove opportunities for agential control contribute to the **entry-level squeeze** by eliminating the foundational tasks that traditionally allowed junior researchers to develop expertise. When AI handles all data interpretation, researchers lose opportunities to:
- Exercise **practical rationality (phronesis)**
- Develop **critical thinking** through trial and error
- Build **confidence in judgment** through responsibility for decisions

## Preserving Agential Control in AI Adoption
Successful firms maintain agential control by:
- Using AI for augmentation rather than replacement
- Preserving human oversight in critical decision points
- Designing systems that explain rather than dictate
- Creating opportunities for researchers to initiate and adjust analysis approaches
