# agential-control

## Definition
Agential control is the ability to adjust the execution of an action as the performance unfolds, a key component of being skilled that distinguishes skills from habits. Skills require control over initiating the task, whereas habits are merely triggered by environmental cues.

## Key Insights
According to Ferdman's 2026 analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:
- To be skilled in something is to have agential control over it, the ability to adjust the execution of the action as it unfolds
- Skills are distinct from habits in an important respect: skills require control over initiating the task, whereas mere habits do not
- An agent who performs out of habit, in response to triggers in the environment, is less skilled compared to the agent who initiated the activity
- As agency develops, the balance between automaticity and control shifts through a process of tuning and attuning

## Connection to AI Systems
AI systems can undermine agential control in several ways:
- Artificial Personal Assistants (APAs) that prompt users to follow pre-determined plans rather than form their own
- Systems that automate routine tasks, depriving users of opportunities to practice judgment
- Recommendation systems that create narrow fields of affordances, limiting opportunities for self-directed action
- AI tools that respond to environmental triggers rather than requiring user initiation

## Implications for Market Research
In market research, maintaining agential control is critical for:
- Preserving researchers' ability to adjust analysis methods as data unfolds
- Ensuring researchers maintain control over interpreting findings rather than following AI-generated conclusions
- Allowing researchers to initiate novel approaches to research questions
- Maintaining the capacity for practical wisdom in research design and execution

## Mitigation Strategies
To preserve agential control in AI-assisted market research:
- Design AI tools that require researcher initiation rather than passive response
- Implement systems that maintain opportunities for researchers to adjust analysis as it unfolds
- Create training that emphasizes the distinction between plan-forming and plan-following
- Ensure AI tools support rather than replace researcher judgment in exceptional circumstances
