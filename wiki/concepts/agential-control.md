# [[agential-control]]

## Definition
The ability to adjust the execution of an action as the performance unfolds, a key component of being skilled that distinguishes skills from habits, as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. Agential control represents the capacity to initiate and modify actions based on ongoing assessment rather than responding automatically to environmental triggers.

## Philosophical Foundation
Ferdman draws on skill philosophy to establish that:
- Skills require control over **initiating** the task
- Habits are merely elicited in response to environmental triggers
- The development of agential control involves a shift in the balance between automaticity and control through a process of tuning and attuning

## Critical Distinction: Plan-Forming vs Plan-Following
The paper introduces a crucial framework for understanding agential control in AI contexts:

| Dimension | Plan-Forming | Plan-Following |
|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| **Agency** | Full agential control | Habitual response |
| **Initiation** | Self-directed | Triggered by environment |
| **Responsibility** | Personal accountability | External attribution |
| **Capacity Development** | Builds cognitive musculature | Atrophies judgment |

As Ferdman states: "To cultivate a skill is to develop flexible links between thought and action, such that continue to evolve even after expertise has been achieved."

## Impact on Market Research Roles
AI systems that encourage **plan-following** rather than **plan-forming** create significant risks for professional development:
- **Entry-level researchers** who only execute pre-determined analysis protocols fail to develop the agential control needed for complex problem-solving
- **AI-powered research tools** that automatically generate insights eliminate opportunities to practice forming research questions and interpretation frameworks
- **Automated reporting systems** that produce finished deliverables deprive analysts of the habituation process necessary for developing judgment

## Evidence from Research
Ferdman cites evidence that "indiscriminately automating human activity may create capacity-hostile environments, insofar as users will be deprived of the habituation process necessary for properly developing the capacities involved in routine activities." This explains why high-intensity AI adopters who maintain opportunities for agential control see 12.0% growth in entry-level roles, while those creating plan-following environments experience the [[entry-level-squeeze]].

## Practical Implementation
Organizations can foster agential control by:
1. Designing AI tools that require human initiation of key processes
2. Creating "red team" opportunities where staff must challenge AI-generated insights
3. Implementing structured reflection sessions after project completion
4. Ensuring junior staff have opportunities to formulate research questions before analysis begins
