# [[agential-control]]

## Core Components
As detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this critical capacity involves:

1. **Initiation**: Ability to self-generate tasks and plans
2. **Adjustment**: Capacity to modify actions during execution
3. **Responsibility**: Taking ownership of planning outcomes

## Threats from AI
- Automated planning systems that eliminate self-deliberation
- Recommendation engines that bypass decision-making
- Workflow automation reducing need for adaptive thinking

## Preservation in Market Research
- Maintain human oversight in AI-assisted research design
- Require manual validation of algorithmic insights
- Structure workflows to preserve decision-making opportunities
- Train researchers in critical evaluation of AI outputs

See [[plan-forming-vs-plan-following]] for related framework.
