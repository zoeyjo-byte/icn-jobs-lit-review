# [[capacity-conducive-environments]]

## Definition

Environments that actively encourage the development and exercise of human capacities through rich opportunities for embodied interaction and shared valuing, providing alternatives to capacity-hostile conditions as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These environments afford and encourage the full maturation of human capacities rather than merely providing opportunities.

## Characteristics

Capacity-conducive environments typically exhibit these features:

- **Wide field of affordances**: Diverse action possibilities that trigger multiple human capacities simultaneously
- **Embodied interaction opportunities**: Spaces designed for physical presence and sensory engagement
- **Intersubjective learning structures**: Mentorship systems that transmit both technical skills and shared valuing of capacities
- **Agential control support**: Systems that preserve opportunities for initiating and adjusting activities
- **Developmental progression**: Pathways that support habituation from novice to expert levels

## Examples in Market Research

- **Hybrid research design workshops**: In-person sessions where teams collaboratively design research approaches, preserving opportunities for plan-forming
- **Mentorship programs with reflection components**: Structured guidance that emphasizes not just 'how' but 'why' certain research practices matter
- **Community spaces for insight sharing**: Physical or virtual environments designed for embodied interaction around shared research challenges
- **AI-augmented rather than AI-automated workflows**: Systems that preserve human decision points in research processes

## Structural Design Principles

Ferdman identifies key design principles for capacity-conducive environments:

> "To ensure the activation of the capacities, the environment has to encourage the activity of φ-ing, otherwise the capacities might not be triggered. This is why the environment conducive to the capacities has to include public goods like community centers, quality public spaces, public libraries, etc. that encourage structured and unstructured embodied interactions. Embodied engagement in these settings would be more conducive to creating shared experiences, which are necessary for shared valuing of the social capacities."

Such environments also afford opportunities for cultivating the **capacity to will** by encouraging agents to develop other capacities without expecting them to be 'virtuous superheroes.'

## Contrast with Capacity-Hostile Environments

Unlike [[capacity-hostile-environments]], which restrict opportunities for capacity development through narrow affordance fields, capacity-conducive environments systematically support the habituation process. They recognize that learning to value capacities is part of their development and create conditions for shared understanding of capacity importance.

## Implementation Challenges

Creating capacity-conducive environments in AI-integrated workplaces requires addressing:

- The **entry-level squeeze** where AI automation eliminates traditional training grounds
- **Value-chain-shift** that repositions where human value is created
- **Human-operating-model-gap** between AI capabilities and human system adaptation
