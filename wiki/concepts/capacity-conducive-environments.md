# [[capacity-conducive-environments]]

## Definition
Environments that actively encourage the development and exercise of human capacities through rich opportunities for embodied interaction and shared valuing, providing alternatives to capacity-hostile conditions as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Characteristics
- Afford structured and unstructured embodied interactions necessary for intersubjective habituation of capacities
- Encourage the development of agential control over capacities
- Support the gradual process of habituation through meaningful practice
- Foster shared understanding of the value of capacities through mentorship
- Provide sufficient action possibilities for developing multiple capacities simultaneously

## Examples in Market Research
- Collaborative workshops where researchers develop skills through hands-on practice with mentorship
- Client-facing roles requiring embodied interaction and moral attention
- Community centers and public spaces that encourage structured and unstructured interactions
- Organizations that maintain quality human-AI collaboration rather than full automation

## Implementation Strategies
Based on [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], capacity-conducive environments require:
- Public goods like community centers, quality public spaces, and public libraries
- Opportunities for embodied interaction that recognize others as moral agents
- Mentorship relationships that instill both mechanical aspects and value of skills
- Systems that encourage rather than merely afford capacity development
- Balance between AI assistance and human agency in professional workflows
