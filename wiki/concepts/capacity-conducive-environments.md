## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Capacity-conducive environments actively encourage the development and exercise of human capacities through rich opportunities for embodied interaction and shared valuing. These environments afford structured and unstructured embodied interactions necessary for intersubjective habituation of capacities.

Key characteristics:
- Provide public goods like community centers, quality public spaces, and public libraries
- Encourage both structured and unstructured embodied interactions
- Create shared experiences necessary for shared valuing of capacities
- Afford opportunities for developing the [[volitional-capacity]] through challenging activities
- Support the full habituation process from novice to expert

Implementation strategies:
- Design physical and digital workspaces that encourage face-to-face collaboration
- Create mentorship programs that facilitate intersubjective learning
- Structure research workflows to preserve opportunities for embodied interaction
- Balance AI automation with human-led activities that require capacity exercise

Market research applications:
- Research firms should design workspaces that encourage embodied interaction among researchers
- Create opportunities for junior researchers to engage in the full research process rather than just executing AI-generated plans
- Develop mentorship programs that transmit not just technical skills but the value of research capacities
- Evaluate AI tools based on whether they preserve opportunities for capacity development
