# Capacity-Conducive Environments

## Definition
Capacity-conducive environments actively encourage the development and exercise of human capacities through rich opportunities for embodied interaction and shared valuing. These environments provide a broad field of affordances that support the cultivation of capacities to their fullest potential.

## Characteristics
- Afford multiple action possibilities for capacity development
- Encourage embodied interaction
- Support intersubjective learning and shared valuing
- Provide opportunities for developing agential control
- Facilitate the habituation process through appropriate challenges

## From Ferdman (2026)
In "AI Deskilling is a Structural Problem," Ferdman emphasizes that capacity-conducive environments:

1. **Encourage rather than merely afford**: "Merely having the opportunity to φ does not guarantee that the agent will actually φ and engage the capacities involved in φ-ing... To ensure the activation of the capacities, the environment has to encourage the activity of φ-ing."

2. **Include public goods**: Ferdman identifies community centers, quality public spaces, and public libraries as examples of public goods that "encourage structured and unstructured embodied interactions."

3. **Support embodied engagement**: These environments create shared experiences necessary for shared valuing of social capacities.

4. **Foster the capacity to will**: They encourage agents to develop and exercise their other capacities by providing appropriate challenges that require willpower.

## Design Principles for Market Research
Based on Ferdman's work, capacity-conducive AI tools in market research should:
- Provide opportunities for researchers to exercise judgment rather than complete automation
- Maintain spaces for embodied interaction among research teams
- Create structured opportunities for mentorship and shared valuing
- Balance automation with challenges that develop researchers' capacities
- Support rather than replace the process of habituation for research skills
