# Capacity-Conducive Environments

## Definition
Environments that actively encourage the development and exercise of human capacities through rich opportunities for embodied interaction and shared valuing, providing alternatives to capacity-hostile conditions as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These environments afford structured and unstructured embodied interactions necessary for intersubjective habituation of capacities.

## Essential Components

### Embodied Interaction
Capacity-conducive environments prioritize physical presence that enables:
- 'Participatory sense-making' where meaning is generated through the interplay between unfolding interaction and participants
- Recognition of others as embodied moral agents through voice and gaze
- Development of moral attention through embodied understanding
- Formation of meaningful relationships that cannot be achieved through disembodied interaction

### Intersubjective Habituation
These environments facilitate capacity development through:
- Mentor-mentee relationships where shared valuing of capacities is cultivated
- Social practices that generate moral reasons for action through constitutive aims
- Community settings that provide opportunities for shared experiences
- Public goods like community centers, libraries, and quality public spaces

### Agential Control Support
They encourage users to exercise control over:
- Initiating activities rather than merely responding to environmental triggers
- Self-deliberation in planning and decision-making
- Adjusting execution of actions as performance unfolds
- Taking responsibility for outcomes to enable self-critique and improvement

## Examples in Market Research
- Research environments where junior analysts work alongside senior colleagues on complex projects
- Client workshops that require embodied interaction and real-time adaptation
- Training programs that scaffold skill development through graduated responsibility
- Communities of practice where researchers share insights and develop shared understanding
- Tools that augment rather than replace human judgment, requiring researchers to justify AI suggestions

## Contrast with Capacity-Hostile Environments
Unlike [[capacity-hostile-environments]], which create narrow fields of affordances, capacity-conducive environments:
- Provide sufficient invitations for valuable activities that trigger capacity development
- Encourage (not just afford) engagement in activities that develop multiple capacities simultaneously
- Support the development of **practical wisdom** through real-world experience
- Foster environments where users become **plan-formers** rather than **plan-followers**

## Implementation Strategies
To create capacity-conducive environments in AI-augmented research:
- Design AI tools that require researchers to explain and justify AI-generated insights
- Structure workflows that maintain human oversight of critical decision points
- Create opportunities for embodied client interaction even when using AI tools
- Develop mentorship programs that explicitly cultivate shared valuing of research capacities
- Ensure AI adoption doesn't eliminate the routine tasks necessary for habituation

## Connection to Human Flourishing
These environments support the neo-Aristotelian concept of [[developmental-perfectionism]], where flourishing is understood as a complex, unified, and well-rounded life achieved through competent exercise of human capacities.
