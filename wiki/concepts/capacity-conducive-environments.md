# [[capacity-conducive-environments]]

## Update 2026-08-26

Capacity-conducive environments actively encourage the development and exercise of human capacities through rich opportunities for embodied interaction and shared valuing, as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These environments afford structured and unstructured embodied interactions necessary for intersubjective habituation of capacities.

### Characteristics

- **Rich Affordance Field**: Provide diverse action possibilities that trigger multiple human capacities simultaneously
- **Embodied Interaction Support**: Create opportunities for physical presence and sensory experience essential for social capacity development
- **Intersubjective Habituation**: Facilitate mentorship relationships where both mechanical aspects and value of skills are transmitted
- **Agential Control Preservation**: Maintain opportunities for initiating and adjusting activities rather than merely responding to environmental triggers
- **Gradual Skill Development**: Support the habituation process through successive trials, mistakes, and refinement

### Implementation Strategies in Market Research

1. **AI-Augmented Rather Than AI-Replaced Processes**: Design research workflows where AI handles routine tasks but preserves opportunities for researchers to develop judgment in complex scenarios

2. **Embodied Research Methods**: Maintain face-to-face qualitative research components even when supplemented with AI analysis tools

3. **Structured Mentorship Programs**: Create formal opportunities for senior researchers to guide juniors through the habituation process, emphasizing not just "how" but "why" certain research approaches have value

4. **Deliberate Skill Development Frameworks**: Implement research project structures that intentionally expose researchers to progressively more complex challenges

5. **Shared Valuing Mechanisms**: Establish team practices that reinforce the intrinsic value of research capacities beyond mere task completion

### Market Research Applications

- **Hybrid Research Design**: Combine AI-powered data collection with human-led interpretation sessions where researchers practice theoretical and practical rationality

- **AI-Assisted Debriefing**: Use AI to generate initial analysis but require researchers to refine and adjust interpretations, preserving agential control

- **Embodied Client Workshops**: Design research engagements that include in-person co-creation sessions, using AI to prepare materials but not replace human interaction

- **Failure Analysis Rituals**: Create team practices for examining research missteps, turning disappointments into habituation opportunities

- **Capacity-Conducive Tool Selection**: Evaluate research technology through Ferdman's framework, prioritizing tools that encourage rather than merely afford capacity development

As Ferdman emphasizes, capacity-conducive environments will afford opportunities for cultivating the [[volitional-capacity]], by encouraging agents to develop and exercise their other capacities without expecting them to be 'virtuous superheroes.' These environments recognize that developing capacities is difficult and requires supportive structures.
