# Habituation Process

## Definition
The gradual process of becoming skilled through practice, involving inculcation through continued attunement to demands of individual cases, with critical implications for AI's impact on skill development. As detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], skill development cannot be learned through testimony alone but requires practice, encountering and overcoming disappointment and failure.

## Key Characteristics

### Gradual Development
Habituation occurs through stages from novice to expert, where:
- Early stages involve more rule-following
- Later stages feature rapid, fluid, involved engagement
- The process itself is one of growth rather than simple repetition
- Competence develops through successive attempts at an activity

### Two Essential Components
1. **Intersubjectivity**: Skill habituation has a 'shared understanding' dimension requiring:
   - Mentors to guide novices into becoming experts
   - Shared valuing of both the skill and its outputs
   - Social practices that generate moral reasons for action

2. **Embodiment**: Successful habituation must include an embodied component where:
   - 'Meaning is generated and transformed in the interplay between unfolding interaction and participants'
   - Embodied interaction enables recognition of others as moral agents
   - Physical presence creates opportunities for 'participatory sense-making'

## AI's Impact on Habituation
Ferdman identifies how AI systems threaten the habituation process:
- Automating routine tasks 'deprives the user of routine opportunities to practice their judgment and strengthen their cognitive musculature'
- Creating environments where users respond to AI outputs as environmental triggers rather than initiating activities
- Reducing embodied interaction necessary for moral and social capacity development
- Offloading planning activities that would otherwise develop practical wisdom

## Examples in Market Research
The **entry-level squeeze** occurs because AI automation eliminates the routine tasks that previously served as:
- Habituation opportunities for junior researchers
- Context for developing practical wisdom through real-world experience
- Foundation for building client relationships through embodied interaction
- Pathway to understanding research quality through successive attempts

## Preserving Habituation in AI Era
Strategies to maintain effective habituation:
- Design AI tools that require researchers to explain and justify AI-generated insights
- Create 'scaffolded' workflows where junior researchers gradually assume responsibility
- Ensure AI adoption doesn't eliminate the routine tasks necessary for skill development
- Structure mentorship programs that explicitly cultivate shared valuing of research capacities
- Maintain opportunities for embodied client interaction even when using AI tools

## Connection to Human Flourishing
The habituation process is essential for developing the **epistemic**, **social**, **creative**, and **volitional capacities** that constitute human flourishing under the [[developmental-perfectionism]] framework. Without proper habituation, researchers risk capacity impoverishment that undermines their professional effectiveness and personal fulfillment.
