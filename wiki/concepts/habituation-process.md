## Update 2026-07-25

Expanded definition based on Ferdman's analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. The habituation process is the gradual development of skill through practice, involving inculcation through continued attunement to demands of individual cases, with critical implications for AI's impact on skill development.

Key aspects:
- Skill development cannot be learned through testimony alone but requires practice
- Involves encountering and overcoming disappointment and failure
- Progresses through stages from novice to expert, with increasing sophistication
- Requires embodied, intersubjective interaction with mentors and peers
- Develops flexible links between thought and action that continue to evolve even after expertise is achieved

In market research contexts, the habituation process is threatened when AI tools:
- Automate routine analytical tasks that previously provided opportunities for judgment development
- Reduce opportunities for researchers to handle unexpected scenarios that build practical wisdom
- Replace the gradual learning process with instant AI-generated insights
