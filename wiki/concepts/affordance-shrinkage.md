# Affordance Shrinkage

**Affordance shrinkage** refers to the systematic, enduring, and deeply entrenched reduction in action possibilities within an environment. It occurs when users are discouraged from finding ways to compensate for the loss of certain capacities, often because the environment itself is designed to make alternative avenues less accessible or appealing.

## Mechanisms
Affordance shrinkage is typically driven by:
- **Automation Bias**: Over-reliance on AI tools leads users to attribute excessive authority to algorithms, reducing critical engagement.
- **Predictable Engagement Loops**: Platforms designed to maximize retention use targeted content, filtered feeds, and predictable triggers that herd users into specific behavioral patterns.
- **Displacement of Practice**: When AI handles routine tasks, users lose the frequency of use required to maintain long-term memory retrieval and judgment skills, leaving them unprepared for exceptions.

## Consequences
A narrow field of affordances normatively problematic because it prevents the triggering of capacities associated with valuable activities. When **[[affordance-shrinkage]]** becomes entrenched, users may not even recognize the loss of deeper competencies, entrenching a shallow level of skilling. This phenomenon is central to the argument that AI-mediated environments can become **[[capacity-hostile]]**, as they structurally inhibit the conditions necessary for capacity cultivation.
