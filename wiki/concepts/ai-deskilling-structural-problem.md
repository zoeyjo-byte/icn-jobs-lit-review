# AI Deskilling Structural Problem

The phenomenon where AI systems undermine human capacities through systemic conditions that create environments hostile to capacity cultivation, as established in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. This should be understood as a structural problem requiring societal solutions, not merely an individual responsibility issue.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Narrow Field of Affordances**: AI interfaces restricting action possibilities
- **Agential Control Erosion**: Systems designed for plan-following rather than plan-forming
- **Habituation Disruption**: Short-circuiting the disappointment/failure cycle essential for skill development
- **Intersubjective Diminishment**: Reducing embodied mentorship opportunities

## Market Research Manifestations
- AI survey tools that prevent methodological experimentation
- Automated reporting systems eliminating interpretation practice
- Chatbots replacing client consultation skill development
