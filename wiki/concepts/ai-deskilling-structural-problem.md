# [[ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]

## Structural Analysis of Deskilling
As established in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this phenomenon should be understood as a structural problem requiring societal solutions rather than merely an individual responsibility issue. The paper identifies:

1. **Capacity-hostile environments** that systematically restrict opportunities for capacity development
2. **Agential control erosion** through automation of planning processes
3. **Intersubjective habituation breakdown** when AI replaces human mentorship
4. **Embodiment challenges** in digital environments

## Key Mechanisms
- **Narrow field of affordances**: AI systems limit action possibilities for capacity exercise
- **Plan-following vs plan-forming**: Automation encourages habitual responses over self-initiated planning
- **Volitional capacity erosion**: Reduced need for effort and perseverance in AI-mediated environments

## Market Research Implications
The structural perspective explains deskilling dynamics in market research workflows where AI tools:
- Automate data analysis tasks that previously developed epistemic capacities
- Replace human interaction in research design that cultivated social capacities
- Encourage reliance on algorithmic recommendations over creative problem-solving
