# [[narrow-field-of-affordances]]

## Definition
An environment that provides limited action possibilities, restricting opportunities for the development and exercise of human capacities, a key mechanism through which AI systems can create capacity-hostile environments as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Limited action possibilities**: Restricts the range of activities users can engage in
- **Systematic reduction**: Affordance shrinkage that is systematic, enduring and deeply entrenched
- **Compensation discouragement**: Discourages users from finding ways to compensate for the shrinkage
- **Cumulative effect**: Results from multiple automated activities across human spheres
- **Capacity restriction**: Limits opportunities for developing specific human capacities

## Examples in Market Research
- AI tools that automate survey design without requiring researchers to engage in cognitive process
- Platforms that generate insights without requiring interpretation skills
- Systems that replace client interaction with digital communication
- Tools that automate data cleaning without providing learning opportunities

## Impact on Capacity Development
As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], narrow fields of affordances lead to:
- Reduced development of [[agential-control]] through limited initiation opportunities
- Impoverished [[habituation-process]] due to lack of meaningful practice
- Diminished [[intersubjectivity-in-skilling]] as AI replaces human mentorship
- Weakened [[embodiment-in-skilling]] when interactions become disembodied

## Identifying Narrow Fields of Affordances
Indicators that an AI system may be creating a narrow field of affordances:
- Users no longer initiate key activities but respond to AI outputs
- Reduced opportunities for encountering and learning from failures
- Diminished need for embodied interaction in professional practice
- Decreased requirement for shared valuing of professional skills

## Mitigation Strategies
To address narrow fields of affordances in market research:
- Design AI tools that provide scaffolding rather than complete solutions
- Create opportunities for practitioners to engage with challenging cases
- Maintain critical embodied interaction points in research workflows
- Balance AI assistance with opportunities for independent practice
- Ensure exposure to diverse cases that challenge developing skills
