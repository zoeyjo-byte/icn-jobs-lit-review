# Narrow Field of Affordances

A narrow field of affordances refers to an environment that provides limited action possibilities, restricting opportunities for the development and exercise of human capacities. As detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this is a key mechanism through which AI systems can create [[capacity-hostile-environments]].

## Mechanisms of Affordance Shrinkage
- **Automation Bias:** When AI tools automate routine tasks, they remove the opportunities for users to practice judgment and strengthen cognitive skills. Users become atrophied and unprepared for exceptions.
- **Systematic & Enduring Reduction:** Affordance shrinkage becomes problematic when it is not temporary but deeply entrenched, discouraging users from finding ways to compensate for the loss of action possibilities.
- **Disembodied Interaction:** Digital mediation fractures the affordance space into disconnected fields (swiping, clicking, scrolling), inviting determinate, separate, and repeatable tasks that fail to mutually inform and enrich cognitive and bodily skills.

## Normative Implications
From a structural perspective, a narrow field of affordances is normatively problematic because it systematically discourages the competent exercise of human capacities. When users are herded into predictable behaviors through targeted algorithms or automated workflows, they lose the invitations to engage in valuable activities that trigger epistemic, social, creative, and volitional capacities.

## Examples in AI Contexts
- **Social Media Platforms:** Filtered newsfeeds and targeted ads maintain predictable engagement patterns, restricting affordances for critical thinking and diverse social interaction.
- **APAs & Productivity Tools:** Constant prompts to accept AI-generated schedules and priorities reduce the affordances for self-deliberation and plan-forming.
- **Healthcare AI:** Over-reliance on diagnostic algorithms reduces the affordances for clinical reasoning and ethical sensitivity among practitioners.

## Counteracting Affordance Shrinkage
Organizations must actively design AI implementations that preserve or expand the field of affordances. This includes maintaining human-in-the-loop workflows, preserving mentorship and embodied collaboration, and ensuring AI tools act as scaffolding rather than replacements.
