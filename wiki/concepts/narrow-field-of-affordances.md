# Narrow Field of Affordances

First observed: 2026-07-17 | Last updated: 2026-07-18

## Definition
A narrow field of affordances is an environment that provides limited action possibilities, restricting opportunities for the development and exercise of human capacities. It is a key mechanism through which AI systems can create capacity-hostile environments as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Key Insights
- Affordances are action possibilities formed by the relationship between an agent and its environment
- A narrow field of affordances not only restricts the number of action possibilities but also discourages users from seeking alternative avenues to compensate
- Affordance shrinkage occurs when the reduction in action possibilities is systematic, enduring, and deeply entrenched
- Narrow fields of affordances can lead to capacity impoverishment by limiting habituation opportunities

## Evidence from Research
As detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:
- "The environment one inhabits is a 'world of affordances'"
- "Affordances are action possibilities formed by a relationship between an agent and its environment offers to persons"
- "A narrow affordance field is normatively problematic... when it leads to 'affordance shrinkage'"
- "Indiscriminately automating human activity may create capacity-hostile environments, insofar as users will be deprived of the habituation process necessary for properly developing the capacities"

## Implications for Market Research
In market research, narrow fields of affordances may emerge when:
- AI tools automate foundational research tasks that traditionally served as training grounds
- Virtual research platforms reduce embodied interaction possibilities
- Standardized research templates limit creative methodology development
- Algorithmic analysis tools reduce opportunities for critical data interpretation

These narrow fields threaten market research capacities by:
- Limiting opportunities for habituation in core research skills
- Reducing the development of practical wisdom through varied research experiences
- Undermining the intersubjective learning that occurs through research collaboration
- Creating environments where research capacities are not collectively valued
