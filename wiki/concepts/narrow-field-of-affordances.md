# Narrow Field of Affordances

## Definition

A narrow field of affordances refers to an environment that provides limited action possibilities, restricting opportunities for the development and exercise of human capacities. This concept is central to understanding how AI systems can create [[capacity-hostile-environments]].

## Key Characteristics

A narrow field of affordances:

1. **Restricts action possibilities**: Limits the range of activities users can engage in
2. **Discourages compensation**: Makes it difficult for users to find alternative ways to exercise capacities
3. **Systematic and enduring**: Represents a deeply entrenched reduction in action possibilities
4. **Cumulative effect**: Multiple narrow affordance fields across different domains compound deskilling risks

## Examples in AI Context

As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], AI systems can create narrow fields of affordances through:

- **Automation bias**: Healthcare AI systems that encourage offloading diagnostic reasoning to machines, reducing opportunities to develop clinical judgment
- **Disembodied interaction**: Social media platforms that fracture users' affordance space into disconnected fields, leading to alienation
- **Personal assistants**: [[artificial-personal-assistants]] that handle routine planning tasks, depriving users of opportunities to "practice their judgment and strengthen their cognitive musculature"

## Structural Implications

The paper emphasizes that narrow fields of affordances represent a structural problem, not merely an individual failing:

> "The problem depicted in these examples is that the affordance space is an impoverished environment: an environment that provides a narrow field of affordances, limiting the opportunities to engage in activities that require the competent exercise of our capacities."

Unlike temporary limitations, a narrow field of affordances becomes problematic when it leads to "affordance shrinkage" - a systematic, enduring reduction in action possibilities that discourages users from finding ways to compensate.

## Contrast with Rich Affordance Fields

A rich field of affordances would:
- Provide diverse opportunities for embodied interaction
- Encourage intersubjective habituation of capacities
- Support the development of agential control
- Allow for failure and learning from mistakes
- Create shared valuing of capacities through community
