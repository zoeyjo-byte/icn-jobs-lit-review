# [[narrow-field-of-affordances]]

## Definition
A narrow field of affordances is an environment that provides limited action possibilities, restricting opportunities for the development and exercise of human capacities, a key mechanism through which AI systems can create capacity-hostile environments as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Key Characteristics
According to Ferdman's analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], narrow fields of affordances have several critical features:

1. **Limited Action Possibilities**: The environment offers fewer opportunities for engaging in activities that trigger human capacities.

2. **Systematic Restriction**: The limitation is not random but systematically restricts certain types of capacities, particularly epistemic, social, moral, and creative capacities.

3. **Discouraged Compensation**: The environment not only restricts opportunities but also discourages users from seeking alternative avenues to compensate for the shrinkage.

4. **Cumulative Effect**: Narrow fields of affordances become particularly problematic when multiple human activities are automated across multiple spheres (writing, searching, planning, organizing, navigation, decision-making).

## Examples
1. **Healthcare AI Systems**: Afford the possibility of offloading tasks to the machine, leading to automation bias where users attribute excessive trust to AI tools, risking the loss of critical skills necessary for unexpected scenarios.

2. **Smartphone Interfaces**: Provide possibilities for interaction that are disconnected from other persons (swiping, clicking, scrolling), fracturing the user's affordance space into disconnected fields, ultimately leading to alienation.

3. **Artificial Personal Assistants**: Constantly available and designed to be helpful, they discourage users from seeking out new opportunities for growth and exploration by replacing most planning activities.

4. **Social Media Platforms**: Create environments that herd users to behave in certain ways through targeted ads and filtered newsfeeds, restricting opportunities for critical thinking and meaningful social interaction.

## Impact on Capacity Development
Narrow fields of affordances undermine human capacity development in several ways:

1. **Shallow Skilling**: They afford only superficial development of capacities without opportunities for mastery.

2. **Capacity Atrophy**: When persons develop capacities in shallow ways, they cannot learn to value more masterful levels of those capacities, leading to capacity atrophy.

3. **Reduced Habituation**: They deprive users of the routine opportunities to practice judgment and strengthen their "cognitive musculature," leaving them unprepared when exceptions arise.

4. **Automation Bias**: They encourage offloading tasks to machines, creating environments where users attribute authority or excessive trust to AI tools over advice from other sources.

## Affordance Shrinkage
Narrow fields of affordances become normatively problematic when they lead to "affordance shrinkage" - when the reduction in action possibilities is systematic, enduring, and deeply entrenched, such that it discourages users from finding ways to compensate for the shrinkage. This is particularly concerning with AI because:

1. **Cumulative Automation**: "Automation and replacement of human activity is becoming more prevalent, across many human spheres."

2. **Discouraged Compensation**: "The more the APA does things for the user, the more it could constitute a capacity-hostile environment... discouraging the user from seeking out new opportunities for growth and exploration."

3. **Structural Nature**: The problem is structural rather than individual - "the context in which a user's behavior occurs can better explain the behavior than appealing to the user's dispositions."

## Implications for Market Research
In the market research industry, narrow fields of affordances could undermine researchers' abilities to:
- Exercise critical thinking when interpreting complex data
- Develop creative approaches to research design
- Build meaningful client relationships through embodied interaction
- Exercise moral judgment in ethical research practices

Organizations should evaluate AI implementations based on whether they create narrow or rich fields of affordances for researchers' capacity development, as outlined in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].
