# Plan-Forming vs Plan-Following

**First Observed:** 2026-07-17  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-25

## Definition

The distinction between actively creating plans through self-deliberation (requiring agential control) versus executing pre-determined plans (more habitual), with critical implications for AI's impact on capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. Expanded with detailed analysis of characteristics, AI's impact, market research implications, and mitigation strategies from Ferdman's research.

## Characteristics

Ferdman (2026) elaborates on this crucial distinction:

### Plan-Forming

> "Forming a plan by self-deliberation requires exercising control over all the steps of the activity of forming the plan, including over initiating the two functions of planning: settling (deciding between options) and coordinating."

- Requires agential control over initiating planning activities
- Involves self-deliberation on values and goals
- Necessitates practical wisdom for coordinating intentions
- Demands creativity for responding to novel situations
- Requires volitional capacity to initiate the planning process

### Plan-Following

> "Relying on an APA to form our plans for us is more like a habit that is developed in response to a trigger in the environment."

- Triggered by environmental cues rather than self-initiated
- Involves responding to pre-determined recommendations
- Lacks the settling function of deciding between options
- Minimal exercise of practical wisdom
- Absence of volitional capacity in initiating planning

## AI's Impact on Planning Capacities

Ferdman identifies how AI tools like Artificial Personal Assistants undermine plan-forming:

> "When much of the planning process is offloaded to the APA, it arguably requires a shallower exercise of the capacities that are ordinarily involved in making coherent plans and executing them."

> "Overreliance on APAs could lead to a reduced sense of personal competence... undermining the habituation of the capacities necessary for trusting one's decisions on life-planning."

## Market Research Implications

This distinction is critical for market research:

- **Research design**: AI tools that generate research plans without researcher input undermine plan-forming capacities
- **Client consultation**: Researchers who merely follow AI-generated recommendations lack the settling function for client needs
- **Problem-solving**: Plan-following researchers struggle with unexpected research challenges
- **Innovation**: Plan-forming capacity is essential for developing novel research approaches

## Mitigation Strategies

Ferdman suggests approaches to preserve plan-forming capacities:

1. **Design for initiation**: Ensure AI tools require researchers to initiate planning activities
2. **Preserve settling function**: Maintain opportunities for researchers to decide between options
3. **Support coordination**: Design tools that assist rather than replace coordination of research elements
4. **Create reflection points**: Build in moments for researchers to analyze their planning decisions
