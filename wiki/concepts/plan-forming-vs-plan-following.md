# [[plan-forming-vs-plan-following]]

## 2026-08-26 Update

The distinction between actively creating plans through self-deliberation (requiring agential control) versus executing pre-determined plans (more habitual), with critical implications for AI's impact on capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

### Plan-Forming
- Involves self-deliberation on values and goals
- Requires exercising theoretical and practical rationality
- Includes both settling function (deciding between options) and coordination function (organizing plans)
- Enables responding to unexpected events and surprises
- Requires agential control over initiating the planning activity
- Involves taking responsibility for plan outcomes
- Facilitates growth through learning from failures

### Plan-Following
- Involves executing pre-determined plans
- Responds to triggers in the environment
- Primarily habitual rather than skill-based
- Limited opportunity for adjustment during execution
- Reduces responsibility for outcomes
- Limits opportunities for growth through failure
- Requires shallower exercise of capacities

### AI's Impact
Ferdman identifies how AI systems convert plan-forming into plan-following:
- **Automated planning tools**: Systems that generate complete plans without user initiation
- **Prescriptive workflows**: Platforms that dictate research methodology without adaptation
- **AI recommendations**: Outputs that users follow without self-deliberation
- **Constant availability**: APAs that prompt users to seek guidance rather than initiate planning

### Market Research Examples
- Researchers using AI-powered research design tools that generate complete methodologies without requiring self-deliberation on research goals
- Automated survey builders that create questionnaires without requiring researchers to engage in the plan-forming process
- AI reporting assistants that generate complete insights without requiring researchers to develop their own interpretations
- Scheduling apps that automatically plan researchers' days without requiring them to initiate the planning process

### Mitigation Strategies
- Design AI tools with initiation points requiring user self-deliberation
- Create workflows preserving opportunities for plan-forming
- Implement reflection stages before accepting AI recommendations
- Develop training emphasizing the value of self-deliberation in research design
- Balance AI assistance with opportunities for independent planning

### Connection to Capacity Development
The plan-forming vs. plan-following distinction is critical for capacity development because:
- Plan-forming requires full exercise of epistemic, social, and creative capacities
- Taking responsibility for plan outcomes enables learning from failures
- Self-deliberation develops practical rationality (phronesis)
- Responding to unexpected events builds creative capacity
- Plan-following encourages shallow skilling while plan-forming supports deep capacity development

Ferdman argues that preserving plan-forming opportunities in market research requires structural solutions that prevent AI systems from completely taking over the planning process. Organizations must design AI tools that support rather than replace the researcher's role as the primary plan-former, recognizing that the capacity to form plans is essential for developing the practical rationality that distinguishes expert researchers. Capacity-conducive research environments maintain spaces where researchers must initiate and adjust their own research plans, even when AI tools provide valuable assistance with execution.
