# [[plan-forming-vs-plan-following]]

## Definition
The plan-forming vs plan-following distinction refers to the critical difference between actively creating plans through self-deliberation (requiring agential control) versus executing pre-determined plans (more habitual), with critical implications for AI's impact on capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Key Characteristics
According to Ferdman's analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this distinction has several critical features:

### Plan-Forming
1. **Agential Control**: Requires exercising control over initiating the activity of forming a plan.
2. **Self-Deliberation**: Involves thoughtful consideration of values, goals, and circumstances.
3. **Two Key Functions**:
   - **Settling Function**: Managing finite cognitive resources when facing choices between options
   - **Coordination Function**: Forming and organizing different types of plans and future-directed intentions
4. **Responsibility Taking**: Involves taking responsibility if the plan fails, affording opportunities for self-criticism and improvement
5. **Practical Wisdom**: Requires practical rationality (phronesis) - doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason

### Plan-Following
1. **Environmental Trigger Response**: Initiated in response to triggers in the environment rather than self-initiated
2. **Habitual Execution**: More like a habit that is developed in response to environmental triggers
3. **Limited Agential Control**: Lacks control over initiating the planning activity
4. **Reduced Responsibility**: When plans fail, responsibility can be shifted to the plan creator (e.g., an AI system)
5. **Shallow Skill Development**: Affords only superficial development of capacities without opportunities for mastery

## Role in Capacity Development
This distinction is fundamental to human capacity development:

1. **Epistemic Capacities**: Plan-forming requires theoretical rationality (thinking, considering reasons to believe) and practical rationality (phronesis).

2. **Social Capacities**: Planning involves coordinating with others toward achieving complex goals, drawing on social relationships for life goals.

3. **Creative Capacities**: Plan-forming requires the ability to reconsider and respond to novel situations, surprises, and unexpected events.

4. **Volitional Capacity**: Plan-forming requires developing and exercising the capacity to will oneself into initiating the activity of forming a plan.

## Impact of AI on Plan-Forming
AI systems, particularly Artificial Personal Assistants, can undermine plan-forming in several ways:

1. **APA Integration**: APAs afford the replacement of many activities involved in both planning and executing plans, which would otherwise require proper cultivation of epistemic, social, moral, and creative capacities.

2. **Trigger-Based Responses**: Users begin by asking the APA for help forming plans, then follow the APA's recommendations, responding to the APA's output as an environmental trigger rather than initiating planning themselves.

3. **Reduced Practical Wisdom**: "Overreliance on APAs could lead to a reduced sense of personal competence... undermining the habituation of the capacities necessary for trusting one's decisions on life-planning."

4. **Responsibility Shifting**: "If an APA makes a plan that ends up failing, the user could more readily shift the responsibility to the APA, forgoing the opportunity for self-growth."

## Implications for Market Research
In the market research industry, preserving plan-forming capabilities is critical for:
- Developing research strategies through self-deliberation rather than following formulaic approaches
- Exercising practical wisdom in research design and interpretation
- Taking responsibility for research outcomes and ethical considerations
- Creating innovative research approaches that respond to novel situations

Organizations should evaluate AI implementations based on whether they encourage plan-forming or promote plan-following behaviors among researchers, as outlined in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].
