# Plan-Forming vs Plan-Following

## Definition
The distinction between **actively creating plans through self-deliberation** (requiring agential control) versus **executing pre-determined plans** (more habitual), with critical implications for AI's impact on capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Philosophical Foundation
Drawing on Bratman's theory of agency, Ferdman explains:
- **Plan-forming** involves two key agential functions:
  * **Settling function**: Managing finite cognitive resources when facing choices
  * **Coordination function**: Forming and organizing different types of plans and intentions
- Plan-forming requires exercising epistemic, social, moral, and creative capacities
- It involves taking responsibility for outcomes and opportunities for self-criticism
- Plan-following, by contrast, involves responding to triggers in the environment

## Critical Distinction
- **Plan-formers** initiate activities, exercise agential control, and develop capacities through self-deliberation
- **Plan-followers** respond to environmental triggers (like AI recommendations), exercising shallower capacities

As Ferdman states: "Forming a plan by self-deliberation requires exercising control over all the steps of the activity of forming the plan... Relying on an APA to form our plans for us is more like a habit that is developed in response to a trigger in the environment."

## Impact on Market Research
AI systems that encourage plan-following rather than plan-forming contribute to:
- The **entry-level squeeze** by eliminating foundational planning opportunities
- Reduced development of **practical rationality (phronesis)**
- Diminished **critical thinking** and **strategic reasoning** capabilities
- Weakened ability to respond to novel research challenges

## Preserving Plan-Forming in AI Adoption
Organizations can maintain plan-forming capabilities by:
- Using AI for suggestions rather than predetermined paths
- Preserving opportunities for researchers to initiate and adjust research approaches
- Creating environments where humans retain control over settling and coordinating functions
- Ensuring AI tools explain rather than dictate research directions
