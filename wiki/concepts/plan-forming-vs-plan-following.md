# [[plan-forming-vs-plan-following]]

## Definition

The distinction between actively creating plans through self-deliberation (requiring agential control) versus executing pre-determined plans (more habitual), with critical implications for AI's impact on capacity development as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Philosophical Foundation

Ferdman develops this distinction using Michael Bratman's theory of agency, explaining that planning is a manifestation of practical rationality with two key functions:

1. **Settling function**: Managing finite cognitive resources when facing choices between options
2. **Coordination function**: Forming and organizing different types of plans and intentions to impose coherence constraints

The critical difference lies in who controls these functions:

> "Forming a plan by self-deliberation requires exercising control over all the steps of the activity of forming the plan, including over initiating the two functions of planning: settling (deciding between options) and coordinating. As such, forming a plan and executing it by settling and coordinating, requires exercising the epistemic, moral, social and creative capacities involved in planning and executing the plan, as well as the capacity to will oneself into initiating the activity of forming a plan to begin with."

> "Relying on an APA to form our plans for us is more like a habit that is developed in response to a trigger in the environment. To illustrate, a user might begin with asking the APA to help them form a plan for finding a soulmate, find a career, resolve a conflict with a friend, or structure their daily schedule. The APA will propose a plan and help the user refine it... In this sequence of events, the user is not initiating the two functions of planning: settling and coordinating, but merely responding to the APAs recommendations."

## Capacity Implications

This distinction has profound implications for human capacity development:

### Plan-Forming Develops Capacities
- Requires **agential control** over initiating planning activities
- Exercises **practical wisdom** in assessing situations and means to ends
- Develops **creative capacity** through responding to novel situations
- Strengthens **volitional capacity** through overcoming planning challenges
- Provides opportunities for **self-criticism** when plans fail

### Plan-Following Diminishes Capacities
- Encourages **habitual response** to environmental triggers
- Reduces opportunities for **practical wisdom** development
- Limits **creative adaptation** to unexpected events
- Undermines **volitional capacity** by removing initiation requirement
- Enables **responsibility shifting** when plans fail

As Ferdman explains:

> "On a similar vein, Schuster and Lazar (2024) argue that responding to some content because it has popped up in my feed is in itself morally problematic, as it is not as good as attending to something for my own moral reasons. Likewise, following a plan because it was suggested by an automated [system]..."

## Artificial Personal Assistants Case Study

The paper uses Artificial Personal Assistants (APAs) as a prime example of technology that encourages plan-following over plan-forming:

> "APAs are constantly available, designed to be helpful, always prompting the user to see if they could be of further assistance. As such, they might discourage the user from seeking out new opportunities for growth and exploration... When the APA replaces most of the user's planning activities, it reduces the user's need to engage in routine, small-scale decision-making about organizing daily activities, or small-scale decisions that over time amount to broader life goals."

Ferdman specifically analyzes scheduling tools like Sunsama:

> "Each morning, Sunsama displays tasks synced from a variety of software, alongside your calendar and unfinished tasks from yesterday. It prompts you to prioritize today's work and to link tasks to your objectives... Sunsama then schedules tasks logically into your next available time slot, intelligently rescheduling them when other tasks run into overtime. The app reminds you throughout the day when it's time to go to a meeting or when you should take a quick break."

## Market Research Implications

This distinction is critical for understanding AI's impact on research roles:

- **Traditional research planning** required plan-forming: designing studies, anticipating challenges, adapting to unexpected findings
- **AI-assisted research** often shifts toward plan-following: implementing pre-configured research templates, following algorithmic recommendations
- **Entry-level roles** increasingly become plan-following positions as AI handles research design
- **Senior roles** must preserve plan-forming opportunities to maintain capacity development pathways

The shift toward plan-following contributes to the **entry-level squeeze** and **human operating model gap** by eliminating traditional capacity development pathways.

## Preserving Plan-Forming Opportunities

Strategies to maintain plan-forming in AI-integrated research environments:

- Design AI systems that present options rather than single recommendations
- Create structured reflection points where researchers must justify AI-generated plans
- Preserve opportunities for researchers to modify research approaches mid-project
- Develop training programs that emphasize the 'why' behind research decisions
- Ensure AI tools support rather than replace the settling and coordination functions of planning
