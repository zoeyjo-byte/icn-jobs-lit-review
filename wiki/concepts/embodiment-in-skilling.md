# embodiment-in-skilling

## Definition
Embodiment in skilling refers to the essential role of physical presence and sensory experience in the development of human capacities, particularly social and moral skills. Being embodied is critical for becoming skilled in social capacities and recognizing others as moral agents.

## Key Insights
According to Ferdman's 2026 analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:
- Successful habituation must include an embodied component
- According to the Enactive Approach in cognition, embodied 'participatory sense-making' is constitutive of social cognition
- The process of habituation of moral attention requires perceiving the other as an embodied being through their voice and gaze
- One comes to apprehend other perceived bodies as being similar loci of embodied subjectivity, creating 'embodied understanding'
- The social interaction itself and its emergent dynamics constitute an essential part of the cognitive process
- Meaning is generated and transformed in the interplay between the unfolding interaction process and the individuals engaged in it

## Importance of Embodiment
Embodiment is critical for:
- Recognizing others as moral agents spontaneously through embodied interaction
- Forming meaningful friendships that don't require excessive discipline
- Developing moral capacities through embodied social cognition
- Creating shared history and participatory sense-making regarding skills
- Triggering the full range of human capacities involved in social interaction

## Connection to AI Systems
AI systems can undermine embodiment in skilling by:
- Creating disembodied environments that make it harder to form meaningful relationships
- Replacing embodied interaction with digital mediation
- Reducing opportunities for spontaneous recognition of others as moral agents
- Creating environments that require more mental effort to attribute moral agency to others
- Diminishing the shared valuing that occurs through embodied interaction

## Implications for Market Research
In market research, embodiment is essential for:
- Developing researchers' capacity for empathy with research participants
- Maintaining the ability to read non-verbal cues in qualitative research
- Preserving the social dimension of research interpretation
- Ensuring researchers develop moral capacities for ethical research practice

## Mitigation Strategies
To protect embodiment in AI-assisted market research:
- Design research methodologies that maintain embodied interaction where possible
- Create opportunities for face-to-face research and team collaboration
- Ensure AI tools don't replace the embodied dimension of research practice
- Develop training that emphasizes the importance of embodied interaction
- Balance AI implementation with preserving spaces for embodied research activities
