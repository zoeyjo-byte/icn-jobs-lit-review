# Embodiment in Skilling

**First Observed:** 2026-07-03  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-25

## Definition

The essential role of physical presence and sensory experience in the development of human capacities, particularly social and moral skills. Embodied interaction is critical for recognizing others as moral agents and forming meaningful relationships, as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. Expanded with detailed analysis of characteristics, contrast with disembodied interaction, threats from AI, preservation strategies, and market research applications from Ferdman's research.

## Characteristics

Ferdman (2026) emphasizes the critical role of embodiment in skill development:

> "Being embodied is critical for becoming skilled insofar as the process of 'shared valuing' of the skill requires some degree of doing something with the mentor(s), creating a shared history and a participatory sense-making regarding both the product of the skill and the skill itself."

Key characteristics:

- **Participatory sense-making**: According to the Enactive Approach, embodied 'participatory sense-making' is constitutive of social cognition
- **Moral recognition**: "Without being embodied in a social interaction—without the return of the gaze of the other—we would be hard-pressed to recognize them spontaneously as moral agents."
- **Spontaneity**: The spontaneity of recognizing others as moral agents is inherent in embodied interaction
- **Shared history**: Embodied interaction creates a shared history necessary for skill transmission

## Contrast with Disembodied Interaction

Ferdman details significant limitations of disembodied interaction:

> "Disembodied interaction requires the agent to exercise more mental efforts in attributing moral agency to the other."

> "For the good of friendship, disembodied interactions make it harder to form meaningful friendship because technologically mediated virtue friendships require more discipline for a diminished experience."

## Threats from AI in Research Contexts

AI tools that promote disembodied interaction threaten market research capacities:

> "The smartphone: it provides possibilities for interaction that are disconnected from other persons, such as swiping, clicking and scrolling. This in turn invites determinate, separate, and repeatable tasks. This fractures the user's affordance space into disconnected fields, ultimately leading to alienation, rather than offering opportunities for the development of new cognitive and bodily skills that are mutually informing and enriching."

Specific threats:
- Remote qualitative research tools that reduce embodied presence with participants
- AI analysis tools that bypass the embodied experience of conducting interviews
- Digital collaboration platforms that limit spontaneous interaction among research teams
- Automated reporting that removes the embodied process of communicating findings

## Preservation Strategies

Ferdman suggests approaches to maintain embodiment in skilling:

1. **Design for embodied interaction**: Create workspaces and tools that facilitate physical co-presence
2. **Balance digital mediation**: Use technology to enhance rather than replace embodied interaction
3. **Preserve fieldwork**: Maintain opportunities for researchers to conduct embodied research activities
4. **Hybrid approaches**: Combine digital tools with embodied practices in research workflows

## Market Research Applications

Embodiment is particularly critical for:
- Qualitative interviewing where physical presence affects data quality
- Observational research that requires embodied presence in contexts
- Team collaboration where spontaneous interaction generates insights
- Client presentations where embodied communication builds trust
