# [[capacity-hostile-environments]]

## Definition

Environments that **restrict, limit, or create a narrow field of affordances** for the development and exercise of core human capacities, as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These environments afford opportunities for shallow skilling while restricting fuller development of capacities, with significant implications for AI's impact on human flourishing.

## Characteristics

- Provide **inadequate conditions** for the development and exercise of human capacities
- Create **systematic, enduring affordance shrinkage** that discourages users from finding compensatory pathways
- Often feature **disembodied interaction** that undermines intersubjective skill development
- Encourage **habitual responses** to environmental triggers rather than agential control
- Restrict opportunities for **embodied interaction** necessary for social and moral skill development

## AI-Specific Examples

- **Artificial Personal Assistants (APAs)** that offload routine planning activities, depriving users of opportunities to "practice their judgment and strengthen their cognitive musculature" (Lee et al. 2025)
- Social media platforms designed to keep users engaged through filtered newsfeeds and information bubbles, creating predictable user behavior patterns
- AI systems that automate routine tasks while leaving only exception-handling to humans, "depriving the user of routine opportunities to practice their judgment"
- Environments that encourage **[[plan-following]]** rather than **[[plan-forming]]**, where users respond to AI-generated recommendations rather than initiating planning activities themselves

## Impact on Capacity Development

Capacity-hostile environments undermine the **[[habituation-process]]** necessary for skill development by:

1. Limiting opportunities for **[[intersubjectivity-in-skilling]]** with mentors and peers
2. Reducing **[[embodiment-in-skilling]]** through disembodied interactions
3. Preventing the development of **[[agential-control]]** over planning and decision-making
4. Creating conditions where users cannot learn to value mastery of capacities through gradual habituation

## Contrast with

- [[capacity-conducive-environments]]: Environments that actively encourage capacity development
- [[narrow-field-of-affordances]]: The specific mechanism through which capacity-hostile environments operate
