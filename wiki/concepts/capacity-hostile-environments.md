# Capacity-Hostile Environments

## Definition
Structural conditions where AI mediation impedes human capacity cultivation, leading to systemic deskilling of core human capacities.

## Key Elements
- **Affordance Shrinkage**: Systematic reduction in action possibilities that discourage capacity development
- **Embodiment Deficit**: Environments lacking physical interaction opportunities critical for skill habituation
- **Intersubjectivity Loss**: Reduced shared valuing of capacities through diminished human interaction

## Examples
- AI automation replacing routine tasks that build cognitive musculature
- Disembodied digital environments limiting social capacity development
- Overreliance on AI personal assistants for life planning
