# [[capacity-hostile-environments]]

## Definition

Environments that restrict, limit, or create a narrow field of affordances for the development and exercise of core human capacities, as analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]. These environments afford opportunities for shallow skilling while restricting fuller development of capacities, with significant implications for AI's impact on human flourishing.

## Characteristics

1. **Narrow field of affordances**: Provides limited action possibilities that restrict opportunities for capacity development
   - Example: Social media platforms that encourage scrolling/swiping rather than embodied interaction
   - Evidence: Butler (2024) shows how smartphone interfaces fracture users' affordance space into disconnected fields

2. **Reduced embodied interaction**: Limits physical presence and sensory experience necessary for social and moral skill development
   - As Ferdman states: "Without being embodied in a social interaction... we would be hard-pressed to recognize [others] spontaneously as moral agents"
   - Implication: Disembodied environments make it "harder to form meaningful friendship because technologically mediated virtue friendships require more discipline for a diminished experience" (Grasso 2025)

3. **Diminished intersubjectivity**: Reduces opportunities for shared valuing of capacities through mentor-mentee relationships
   - Critical for habituation: "Becoming skilled typically requires a mentor to guide the novice... instills not only mechanical aspects but also the value of the skill"
   - Social dimension: "Social practices play an important role in the formation of shared valuing regarding both the outputs and the skill itself"

## AI Manifestations

| AI System | Capacity-Hostile Mechanism | Impact |
|-----------|----------------------------|--------|
| [[artificial-personal-assistants]] | Offloading life-planning activities | Undermines development of practical wisdom and volitional capacity |
| Healthcare AI | Automation bias in diagnostic reasoning | Diminishes clinical judgment, reduces retention of tacit knowledge |
| Recommendation systems | Creating filter bubbles | Undermines epistemic capacities through limited exposure to diverse perspectives |

## Market Research Implications

Capacity-hostile environments explain the structural nature of the [[human-operating-model-gap]]. When AI tools in market research:
- Automate foundational analysis tasks without providing embodied learning opportunities
- Replace human interaction in client relationships with AI-mediated communication
- Create narrow fields of affordances for junior researchers

...they contribute to the [[entry-level-squeeze]] by eliminating the habituation process necessary for developing professional expertise. This structural perspective shows why simply training workers on AI tools is insufficient - the work environment itself must be redesigned to be capacity-conducive.
