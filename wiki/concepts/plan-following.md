# [[plan-following]]

## Definition
Plan-following refers to the execution of pre-determined plans without exercising agential control over the planning process itself. As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this represents a habitual mode of action where individuals respond to environmental triggers rather than initiating and adjusting plans through self-deliberation.

## Key Characteristics
- Responding to triggers in the environment rather than initiating activity
- Lacking control over the 'settling function' (deciding between options)
- Lacking control over the 'coordination function' (organizing different plans)
- Removing responsibility for plan outcomes
- Limiting opportunities for practical wisdom development
- Becoming acclimated through repeated exposure to automated planning

## Contrast with Plan-Forming
| **Plan-Forming** | **Plan-Following** |
|------------------|--------------------|
| Involves self-deliberation on values and goals | Responds to environmental triggers |
| Exercises theoretical and practical rationality | Follows predetermined patterns |
| Takes responsibility for plan outcomes | Shifts responsibility to external agents |
| Develops practical wisdom through experience | Limits practical wisdom development |
| Requires agential control over initiating activity | Lacks control over initiating activity |

## AI's Role in Encouraging Plan-Following
- APAs provide complete plans that users merely execute
- Research platforms that schedule tasks intelligently remove planning needs
- Automated insight generation systems execute predetermined analysis
- Constant APA availability discourages seeking new growth opportunities
- Over-reliance on APAs leads to reduced sense of personal competence

## Market Research Implications
- AI tools that provide predetermined research plans undermine researcher expertise
- Automated reporting systems execute fixed templates without researcher input
- Plan-following environments limit opportunities for developing research wisdom
- Diminished opportunities for responding to unexpected research findings
- Reduced capacity for creative adaptation to changing market conditions

## Mitigation Strategies
- Design research tools preserving opportunities for researcher plan-forming
- Create hybrid workflows that balance AI assistance with human planning
- Implement structured reflection on research values and goals
- Evaluate AI research tools based on their support for agential control
- Establish 'planning rituals' that maintain researcher initiative
