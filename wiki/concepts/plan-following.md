# [[plan-following]]

## Definition

Plan-following refers to the execution of pre-determined plans without exercising agential control over the planning process itself. As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this represents a habitual mode of action where individuals respond to environmental triggers rather than initiating and adjusting plans through self-deliberation.

## Key Characteristics

1. **Environment-triggered**: Actions initiated in response to external triggers rather than self-deliberation
2. **Habitual nature**: "Relying on an APA to form our plans for us is more like a habit that is developed in response to a trigger in the environment"
3. **Reduced responsibility**: "If an APA makes a plan that ends up failing, the user could more readily shift the responsibility to the APA"
4. **Shallow skilling**: Requires "a shallower exercise of the capacities that are ordinarily involved in making coherent plans and executing them"
5. **Deskilling risk**: Undermines opportunities for capacity development through reduced agential control

Ferdman explains: "One of the features of being skilled is having agential control over initiating a task, whereas fulfilling the task in response to a trigger from the environment is more like a habit than a skill. A habitual activity is an activity that is triggered by something in the environment, where the agent performs the activity in response to the trigger."

## Contrast with Plan-Forming

| Feature | Plan-Following | Plan-Forming |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Initiation | Environment-triggered | Self-initiated |
| Control | Limited agential control | Full agential control |
| Responsibility | Diffused | Owned |
| Capacity development | Limited | Rich |
| Cognitive engagement | Shallow | Deep |

Ferdman states: "Forming a plan by self-deliberation requires exercising control over all the steps of the activity of forming the plan, including over initiating the two functions of planning: settling (deciding between options) and coordinating."

## AI's Role in Encouraging Plan-Following

Artificial Personal Assistants (APAs) promote plan-following through:
- Constant availability: "APAs are constantly available, designed to be helpful, always prompting the user to see if they could be of further assistance"
- Routine task replacement: "When the APA replaces most of the user's planning activities, it reduces the user's need to engage in routine, small-scale decision-making"
- Reduced competence: "Overreliance on APAs could lead to a reduced sense of personal competence"

As Ferdman notes: "The APA will propose a plan and help the user refine it, according to their personal preferences or past behavior, and the user will then follow the plan. In this sequence of events, the user is not initiating the two functions of planning: settling and coordinating, but merely responding to the APAs recommendations."

## Implications for Market Research

Plan-following is problematic in market research because:
- Research design requires self-initiated planning to address novel challenges
- Ethical decision-making depends on personal responsibility rather than diffused accountability
- Creative methodology development emerges from plan-forming
- Client relationship management requires adaptive planning rather than rigid execution

AI tools that automate research planning risk:
- Creating environments where researchers become plan-followers rather than plan-formers
- Undermining opportunities to develop practical wisdom through self-deliberation
- Reducing researchers' ability to handle unexpected research challenges
- Diminishing the volitional capacity needed to initiate research directions

## Mitigation Strategies

To counter plan-following in AI-augmented market research:
- Design AI tools that support rather than replace research planning
- Create opportunities for researchers to initiate research directions
- Ensure AI outputs are presented as suggestions requiring human approval
- Maintain human oversight for ethical considerations in research design
- Develop evaluation frameworks that value creative research planning
