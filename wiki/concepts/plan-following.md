# Plan-Following

Plan-following refers to the execution of pre-determined plans without exercising agential control over the planning process itself. As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this represents a habitual mode of action where individuals respond to environmental triggers rather than initiating and adjusting plans through self-deliberation. It stands in direct contrast to [[plan-forming-vs-plan-following|plan-forming]].

## Characteristics
- **Trigger-Response Mechanism:** Actions are elicited by external cues (e.g., AI recommendations, algorithmic suggestions) rather than internal deliberation.
- **Reduced Agential Control:** The user does not initiate the activity or adjust its execution based on unfolding contexts. Control is ceded to the external system.
- **Responsibility Diffusion:** When plans fail, users can more readily shift blame to the AI tool, avoiding self-criticism and the opportunities for growth that come from analyzing failure.
- **Shallow Capacity Exercise:** Requires less epistemic, moral, social, and creative engagement than plan-forming, leading to atrophied judgment and practical wisdom.

## Role of AI in Encouraging Plan-Following
APAs and automated planning tools are designed to be constantly available and helpful, inviting users to offload planning activities. Features like daily task scheduling, priority setting, and calendar optimization prompt users to accept AI-generated plans rather than formulate their own. This constant availability limits the user's ability to develop independence and undermines the habituation of capacities necessary for trusting one's own decisions.

## Mitigation Strategies
- **Mandate Human Initiation:** Require researchers and analysts to draft initial plans, hypotheses, or strategies before leveraging AI for refinement.
- **Design for Reflection:** Build prompts and interfaces that ask users to articulate their reasoning, values, and goals before accepting AI suggestions.
- **Preserve Exception Handling:** Ensure humans remain responsible for edge cases and system failures to maintain judgment and cognitive agility.
