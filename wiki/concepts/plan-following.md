# Plan-Following

## Definition
Plan-following refers to the execution of pre-determined plans without exercising agential control over the planning process itself. As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this represents a habitual mode of action where individuals respond to environmental triggers rather than initiating and adjusting plans through self-deliberation.

## Key Characteristics

### Habitual Nature
- Executing plans constructed by others rather than forming one's own
- Responding to triggers in the environment rather than initiating activity
- Lacking control over the 'settling' function (deciding between options)
- Absence of the capacity to will oneself into initiating the planning activity

### Contrast with Plan-Forming
Plan-following differs fundamentally from plan-forming:
- **Plan-forming**: Involves self-deliberation, agential control, and responsibility for outcomes
- **Plan-following**: Involves responding to pre-determined recommendations without initiating the planning process

As Ferdman explains, 'forming a plan by self-deliberation requires exercising control over all the steps of the activity of forming the plan, including over initiating the two functions of planning: settling (deciding between options) and coordinating.' In contrast, 'relying on an APA to form our plans for us is more like a habit that is developed in response to a trigger in the environment.'

## AI's Role in Promoting Plan-Following
Artificial Personal Assistants (APAs) and other AI tools encourage plan-following by:
- Providing constant recommendations that users respond to as environmental triggers
- Designing interfaces that 'prompt you to prioritize today's work' and 'schedule tasks logically'
- Creating environments where users 'respond to the APA's recommendations' rather than initiating planning
- Offering 'help me plan my day' and 'how should I fill an unscheduled hour' functionality that replaces self-deliberation

## Capacity Deskilling Effects
Plan-following contributes to capacity deskilling by:
- Reducing opportunities to develop practical wisdom through real-world experience
- Diminishing the habituation process necessary for skill development
- Undermining the development of agential control over capacities
- Creating environments where users 'are not initiating the two functions of planning: settling and coordinating'
- Reducing opportunities to take responsibility for outcomes and learn from mistakes

## Market Research Examples

### AI-Powered Research Tools
- Systems that generate research questions and methodology without researcher input
- Platforms that automate client reporting with minimal human oversight
- Tools that offload analytical reasoning to AI without requiring researchers to justify conclusions

### Workflow Automation
- Scheduling tools that automatically assign research tasks
- Project management systems that dictate research workflows
- AI assistants that provide step-by-step guidance through research processes

## Structural Solutions
To counteract plan-following tendencies in AI-augmented research:
- Design tools that require researchers to explain and justify AI-generated insights
- Structure workflows to maintain human oversight of critical planning decisions
- Create 'red team' processes where alternative research approaches are deliberately sought
- Ensure AI tools support rather than replace the researcher's role as ultimate decision-maker
- Develop training that emphasizes the researcher's responsibility for planning and execution

## Connection to Capacity-Hostile Environments
Plan-following is a key mechanism through which AI systems create **capacity-hostile-environments**:
- It represents a narrow field of affordances that discourages users from seeking alternative pathways
- It undermines the intersubjective habituation process necessary for capacity development
- It reduces opportunities for embodied interaction in planning activities
- It diminishes the volitional capacity required to initiate and adjust plans

## Contrast with Plan-Forming
Unlike plan-forming environments that support **capacity-conducive-environments**, plan-following environments:
- Restrict opportunities for developing practical wisdom
- Diminish the habituation process through reduced practice
- Create conditions where users become dependent on external guidance
- Undermine the development of agential control over capacities
