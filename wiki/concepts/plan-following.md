## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Plan-following refers to the execution of pre-determined plans without exercising agential control over the planning process itself. As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this represents a habitual mode of action where individuals respond to environmental triggers rather than initiating and adjusting plans through self-deliberation.

Characteristics:
- Triggered by something in the environment (e.g., AI recommendations)
- Lacks control over initiating the planning activity
- Absent the volitional capacity to initiate planning
- May lead to shifting responsibility when plans fail
- Does not require the full exercise of epistemic, social, and creative capacities

Contrast with plan-forming:
- Plan-forming involves self-deliberation on values and goals
- Requires exercising theoretical and practical rationality
- Involves taking responsibility for plan outcomes
- Enables self-criticism and improvement when plans fail

AI's role:
- Artificial Personal Assistants afford the replacement of plan-forming with plan-following
- Over-reliance on AI for life-planning reduces opportunities to develop practical wisdom
- APA convenience and constant availability may limit agents' ability to transition to independent planning

Market research implications:
- Research firms should evaluate whether their AI tools encourage plan-following
- Junior researchers need opportunities to develop research plans rather than just execute them
- AI tools should be designed to scaffold rather than replace the plan-forming process
- Research methodologies should preserve opportunities for researchers to exercise agential control over planning
