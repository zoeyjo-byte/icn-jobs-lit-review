# [[volitional-capacity]]

## Definition
The human capacity to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow in order to achieve intrinsically valuable goals, treated as a meta-capacity necessary for developing all other capacities. As detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this capacity is essential for human flourishing and is particularly threatened by AI-mediated environments.

## Key Characteristics
- Exerting effort toward intrinsically valuable goals
- Overcoming difficulty and persevering through challenges
- Growing through the process of capacity development
- Required for initiating and sustaining plan-forming activities
- Functions as a meta-capacity underlying all other capacities

## Connection to Capacity Development
- Developing any human capacity requires willpower and determination
- Capacity cultivation involves encountering and overcoming disappointment
- Habituation process requires sustained effort over time
- Plan-forming requires will to initiate the planning activity
- Without volitional capacity, other capacities remain undeveloped

## Market Research Implications
- Essential for persevering through complex research challenges
- Critical for developing research expertise through sustained effort
- Necessary for maintaining research integrity in difficult situations
- Fundamental to overcoming research obstacles and setbacks
- Required for the long-term development of research excellence

## Threats from AI
- Capacity-hostile environments that discourage exercise of willpower
- AI tools that replace human activity with automated solutions
- Plan-following environments that remove need for initiating activities
- Reduced opportunities for overcoming difficulty and learning from failure
- Narrow fields of affordances that limit growth opportunities

## Preservation Strategies
- Design research workflows that preserve opportunities for willful effort
- Create 'challenge-friendly' environments that value overcoming difficulty
- Balance AI assistance with opportunities requiring sustained effort
- Implement structured reflection on research goals and values
- Evaluate AI research tools based on their support for volitional development
