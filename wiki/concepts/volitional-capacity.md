# [[volitional-capacity]]

## Definition
Volitional capacity is the human capacity to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow in order to achieve intrinsically valuable goals, treated as a meta-capacity necessary for developing all other capacities. As established in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], this capacity is essential for human flourishing and can be undermined by AI systems through reduced opportunities for effortful engagement.

## Key Characteristics
Ferdman explains that volitional capacity involves:
- The ability to exert effort in the face of difficulty
- Perseverance through challenges and setbacks
- Growth through overcoming obstacles
- Commitment to intrinsically valuable goals
- Self-discipline to initiate and sustain effortful activities

## Meta-Capacity Role
Ferdman treats volitional capacity as a meta-capacity because:
- Developing and exercising any human capacity is difficult
- The competent exercise of other capacities requires volitional effort
- Without volitional capacity, other capacities cannot be developed or maintained
- It enables the cultivation of agential control over other capacities

## Role in Life-Planning
Ferdman emphasizes that volitional capacity is essential for life-planning:
- Life-planning requires the capacity to will oneself into initiating the activity of planning
- Resisting the temptation to outsource planning to external entities requires volitional strength
- Overcoming obstacles in executing life plans requires perseverance
- Developing practical wisdom requires sustained effort over time

## Implications for AI Systems
Ferdman argues that AI systems can undermine volitional capacity through:
- Reducing the need to exercise willpower by automating effortful activities
- Creating environments that discourage users from seeking avenues to compensate for deskilling
- Providing constant assistance that removes opportunities for perseverance
- Narrowing the field of affordances for developing effortful capacities

## Professional Context
In market research, volitional capacity manifests as:
- Perseverance through challenging research projects
- Commitment to ethical research practices despite pressures
- Effort required to develop practical wisdom in research design
- Growth through overcoming research obstacles and failures
- Self-discipline to maintain research quality standards

## Capacity Development
Volitional capacity develops through:
- Environments that provide appropriately challenging activities
- Opportunities to experience and learn from failure
- Mentorship that encourages growth through difficulty
- Settings that require self-initiated effort rather than passive consumption
