## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Volitional capacity is the human capacity to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow in order to achieve intrinsically valuable goals. It is treated as a meta-capacity necessary for developing all other capacities.

Key characteristics:
- Required for developing and exercising all other human capacities
- Involves the ability to will oneself into initiating activities
- Critical for resisting the temptation to outsource activities to external entities
- Developed through overcoming challenges and persisting through difficulty

Connection to capacity development:
- Cultivating any capacity requires effort and determination
- The volitional capacity enables agents to initiate plan-forming rather than defaulting to plan-following
- Without volitional capacity, agents become dependent on external triggers rather than initiating activities

Threats from AI:
- AI tools that automate routine tasks reduce opportunities to exercise volitional capacity
- Over-reliance on AI for decision-making undermines the ability to initiate activities
- Convenience of AI tools may erode perseverance in facing research challenges

Market research applications:
- Research firms should design workflows that preserve opportunities to exercise volitional capacity
- Junior researchers need challenging assignments that require perseverance
- AI tools should be implemented in ways that don't eliminate the need for effortful research activities
- Career development should focus on building volitional capacity alongside technical skills
