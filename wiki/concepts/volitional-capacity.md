# Volitional Capacity

The **volitional capacity** is the human ability to exert effort, overcome difficulty, persevere, and grow in order to achieve intrinsically valuable goals. It functions as a meta-capacity, as developing and exercising any other core human capacity requires willpower and determination.

## Role in Flourishing
Under **[[developmental-perfectionism]]**, the competent exercise of capacities is difficult and often requires sustained effort. The volitional capacity is what enables agents to initiate activities, resist temptation, and push through the discomfort inherent in skill acquisition. Without it, individuals are unlikely to transition from novice to expert stages of **[[embodied-habituation]]**.

## AI and the Erosion of Will
AI tools that offer constant convenience and offload decision-making risk undermining the volitional capacity. When environments afford users the opportunity to avoid effortful engagement, they discourage the exercise of willpower. This creates a feedback loop where the lack of practice in perseverance further weakens the capacity, making it harder to cultivate other skills. Capacity-conducive environments, by contrast, encourage the volitional capacity without expecting agents to be "virtuous superheroes," by designing systems that invite effortful engagement rather than bypassing it.
