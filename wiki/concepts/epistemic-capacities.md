# epistemic-capacities

## Definition
Epistemic capacities are the human capacities related to knowledge, reasoning, and understanding, including theoretical rationality and practical rationality (phronesis), which are essential for navigating truth and making informed decisions.

## Key Insights
According to Ferdman's 2026 analysis in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:
- Epistemic capacities include theoretical rationality: thinking, considering reasons to believe, forming beliefs, contemplating
- Epistemic capacities also include practical rationality (phronesis/common sense or wisdom): doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason
- Practical wisdom is necessary for critically reflecting on conceptions of a flourishing life, setting appropriate goals, and striving to accomplish them correctly
- A wise person knows effective means to ends and possesses accurate understanding of situations and circumstances
- Practical wisdom involves discernment: correct understanding of how the good and bad apply in particular circumstances

## Connection to AI Systems
AI systems can undermine epistemic capacities by:
- Creating environments where users rely on AI outputs rather than developing their own reasoning
- Reducing opportunities for users to practice theoretical rationality through self-deliberation
- Automating analysis tasks that would otherwise develop practical wisdom
- Creating capacity-hostile environments that narrow the field of affordances for epistemic development
- Encouraging plan-following rather than plan-forming, which requires practical rationality

## Implications for Market Research
In market research, epistemic capacities are essential for:
- Developing theoretical rationality to form accurate beliefs from research data
- Exercising practical wisdom in research design and interpretation
- Making sound judgments about research validity and applicability
- Critically reflecting on research goals and methodologies
- Setting appropriate research objectives and accomplishing them effectively

## Mitigation Strategies
To protect epistemic capacities in AI-assisted market research:
- Design AI tools that support rather than replace researcher reasoning
- Create opportunities for researchers to practice theoretical rationality through self-deliberation
- Maintain spaces for developing practical wisdom through real-world research experience
- Implement training that emphasizes the development of phronesis in research contexts
- Ensure AI implementation doesn't automate the foundational tasks necessary for epistemic capacity development
