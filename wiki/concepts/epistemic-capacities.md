# [[epistemic-capacities]]

## Definition
Epistemic capacities are the human capacities related to knowledge, reasoning, and understanding, including theoretical rationality and practical rationality (phronesis), which are essential for navigating truth and making informed decisions. As detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], these capacities are constitutive of human flourishing rather than merely instrumentally valuable.

## Two Key Dimensions
Ferdman's analysis identifies two fundamental aspects of epistemic capacities:

### Theoretical Rationality
- Thinking, considering reasons to believe, forming beliefs, contemplating
- Critical for knowledge acquisition and truth evaluation
- Enables understanding of complex concepts and relationships
- Requires engagement with evidence and logical reasoning

### Practical Rationality (Phronesis)
- Common sense or wisdom: doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason
- Involves accurate understanding of one's situation and circumstances
- Requires discernment: correct understanding of how the good and bad apply in particular contexts
- Essential for critically reflecting on one's conceptions of a flourishing life

## Development Process
Epistemic capacities develop through:

- **Habituation**: A gradual process requiring practice, encountering and overcoming disappointment and failure
- **Intersubjective Learning**: Requires mentorship to instill not only mechanical aspects but also the value of epistemic capacities
- **Embodied Interaction**: Benefits from physical presence and sensory experience in learning environments
- **Agential Control**: Requires the ability to initiate and adjust epistemic activities rather than merely responding to environmental triggers

## Threats from AI Systems
Ferdman identifies several ways AI systems can undermine epistemic capacities:

- **Critical Thinking Erosion**: Evidence of "growing evidence of the erosion of critical thinking" as a result of over-reliance on AI
- **Decision-Making Impairment**: Reduction in "decision-making and analytical reasoning" capabilities
- **Automation Bias**: When users attribute "authority or excessive trust to an AI tool over advice from other sources"
- **Shallow Engagement**: Environments that afford "opportunities for shallow skilling" rather than deep epistemic development

## Implications for Market Research
For the market research industry, epistemic capacities are particularly critical because:

- Research excellence depends on strong theoretical and practical rationality
- Analytical reasoning is fundamental to transforming data into insights
- Critical thinking is essential for evaluating research methodologies and findings
- The [[entry-level-squeeze]] threatens traditional pathways for developing epistemic capacities
- The [[human-operating-model-gap]] reflects a mismatch between AI capabilities and epistemic development needs
