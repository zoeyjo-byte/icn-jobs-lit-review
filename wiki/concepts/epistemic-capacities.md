## Update (2026-08-20)

Expanded with detailed analysis from [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]]:

Epistemic capacities include theoretical rationality (thinking, considering reasons to believe, forming beliefs, contemplating) and practical rationality (phronesis or wisdom) - doing the right thing in the right way for the right reason.

Key components:
- Theoretical rationality: thinking, considering reasons, forming beliefs, contemplating
- Practical rationality (phronesis): wisdom in determining effective means to ends, accurate understanding of situations, discernment in applying good/bad to circumstances
- Requires real-world experience to develop fully

Threats from AI in market research:
- Over-reliance on AI for data analysis may erode researchers' ability to exercise theoretical rationality
- AI-generated insights may reduce opportunities for researchers to develop practical wisdom
- Automated reporting tools may encourage "plan-following" rather than critical evaluation
- Constantly turning to AI for advice restricts real-world experience necessary for wisdom development

Preservation strategies:
- Maintain human review of AI-generated insights
- Encourage researchers to articulate their own interpretations before consulting AI
- Design research workflows that preserve opportunities for epistemic skill development
- Create opportunities for researchers to learn from mistakes in interpretation
