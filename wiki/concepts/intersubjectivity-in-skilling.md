# [[intersubjectivity-in-skilling]]

## Definition
The shared understanding dimension of skill development, where learning occurs through relationships with others who guide and share the value of the skill. Becoming skilled typically requires a mentor to instill not only mechanical aspects but also the value of the skill, as detailed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Mentorship requirement**: Becoming skilled typically requires a mentor to guide the novice
- **Shared valuing**: Mentor instills not only mechanical aspects but also the value of the skill
- **Shared intentions**: Mentor and mentee develop shared intentions regarding the skill
- **Social practices**: Groups and societies provide mentoring environments through social practices
- **Constitutive aim**: Social practices have a telos of helping people fare well rather than badly

## Importance in Market Research
Intersubjectivity is essential for developing:
- Research ethics through shared understanding of moral responsibilities
- Client relationship skills through embodied interaction
- Analytical judgment through shared interpretation of complex data
- Professional identity through community of practice

## Threats from AI Systems
As analyzed in [[ferdman-2026-ai-deskilling-structural-problem]], AI can undermine intersubjectivity by:
- Replacing human mentorship with algorithmic guidance
- Reducing opportunities for shared valuing of research skills
- Creating disembodied interactions that limit shared understanding
- Diminishing the social practices that generate moral reasons for action

## Preserving Intersubjectivity in AI Environments
Strategies to maintain intersubjective skill development:
- Design AI tools that complement rather than replace human mentorship
- Create structured opportunities for shared interpretation of AI outputs
- Maintain embodied interaction spaces for developing shared understanding
- Foster communities of practice that share values around research skills
- Balance AI assistance with opportunities for human-to-human learning
