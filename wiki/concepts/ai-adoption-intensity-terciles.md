# AI Adoption Intensity Terciles

## Definition
AI Adoption Intensity Terciles is a methodology developed in the [[ramp-revelio-2026-ai-jobs-impact-study]] to categorize firms based on the intensity of their AI adoption, measured by the PEPM (AI Spend Per Employee) metric.

## Methodology
1. Firms are classified as AI adopters when they have three consecutive months of at least $100 in AI vendor spending
2. Adoption intensity is measured as monthly AI spend per employee (PEPM) over the first three months after adoption
3. Among treated firms, PEPM values are sorted and divided into three equal groups (terciles):
   - High intensity: top tercile (most intensive AI adopters)
   - Medium intensity: middle tercile
   - Low intensity: bottom tercile (least intensive AI adopters)
4. For analysis, firms are often grouped as:
   - High intensity: top tercile
   - Low intensity: bottom two terciles combined

## Rationale for Tercile Approach
- Allows researchers to examine differential impacts of AI adoption based on intensity
- Reveals that not all AI adopters experience the same employment effects
- Helps address selection bias by comparing firms with similar eventual adoption intensity
- Shows that substantial employment gains are concentrated among high-intensity adopters

## Key Findings Using Intensity Terciles
- High-intensity adopters grew total employment by 10.2% following adoption
- Entry-level headcount rose 12.0% for high-intensity adopters
- Low-intensity adopters showed no statistically significant change in employment
- High-intensity gains emerged gradually over 18-24 months, suggesting a learning curve
- Employment gains were broad across roles for high-intensity adopters (engineering, sales, administration, customer service)

## Significance
This methodology revealed a critical insight often missed in prior research: AI's labor market effects differ substantially based on adoption intensity. Studies that treat all adopters the same may miss these nuanced patterns.
