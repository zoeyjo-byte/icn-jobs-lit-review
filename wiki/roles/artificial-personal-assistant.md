# [[artificial-personal-assistant]]

## Role Description
AI systems that perform life-planning and organizational tasks including:
- Daily scheduling
- Goal setting
- Decision-making support
- Social interaction mediation

## Capacity Impact
- Creates plan-following habits vs plan-forming skills
- Reduces need for practical wisdom development
- Limits embodied interaction opportunities

## Case Studies
- Sunsama scheduling app
- ChatGPT life coaching
- Gemini personal organization
